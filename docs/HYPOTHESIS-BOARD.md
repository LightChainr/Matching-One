## Reinterpretation rules

- Exact contact closure reclassifies `gamma_D4`; it does not negate the observed finite response or the utility of the marked stream.
- The completed mean-line and two-radius local tests reject their declared q2 carriers; they do not reject ordinary global H4, external Euler coupling, charged four-leg or every separated landing observer.
- Annulus R1 failure is sector-specific. It does not imply that every plus/minus row needs the same rank-two generator.
- The first shared-context rectangle supports one effective generator only for its local pivotal rows. P200 and the rectangle still lack `h0,h2,h5,h25` or both path orders, so they do not identify chronology.
- Descriptor `migration_required` means future semantic transport is not yet machine-checked. It is not an error label, evidence demotion or reason to block a mathematically distinct analysis.
- Fixed-rank motif contrasts are exact-zero controls, not explanations of `p_c` or proof of useful variance reduction.
- Main's rank-four mixing audit rejects an assumed amplitude closure, not the exact parity zero pattern or the four powers.
- P321's completed fresh pass is compatibility, not thermal-Q4 identification. The next typed sector/independent-projector row asks a different question; ordinary shape replication does not prove transparency.
- A raw Q tangent is not defined by its Q=1 endpoint alone. P333's lift counterterm is exact transport data, not Jordan evidence before bottom-field projection and field connection.
- P158's sample counts are a source-only power forecast, not N650/N850 observations. They lower current production value without forbidding a target variance pilot or future redesigned estimator.
- Issue55 production is now complete: H4-only and zero both survive and A12 is unresolved. This lowers the value of the third alias under the same weak source; it does not rule out H12 under a stronger source.
- A lower-ranked route stays open and can immediately rise when it offers a sharper observer, exact map or shared acquisition.
