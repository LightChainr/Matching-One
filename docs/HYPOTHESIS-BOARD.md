## Parallel method lane and six-coordinate dashboard

Proof-carrying elimination is a high-value method lane for small declared classes, not a serial prerequisite. A claim-bearing SOS/moment exclusion requires bounded or Archimedean support, complete similarity-gauge coverage, explicit singular-covariance handling, a frozen outer confidence set and exact or interval certificate reconstruction. A numerical relaxation optimum is diagnostic, and an inconclusive relaxation neither demotes the model nor blocks exploratory work.

The Operator Passport is a dashboard with six separable coordinates: representation/projector, finite algebra, propagation, sphere fusion, torus/modulus and microscopic control. Any coordinate may redirect the next experiment; “passport complete” is not a status, gate or admission checklist.

The literature boundary remains narrow. Normalization-free loop-model three-point/OPE ratios and torus one-point modulus ratios are candidate discriminators for Matching One, not proven fingerprints for its current lattice observable or arbitrary Jordan/defect fields. Exact finite-lattice singular-vector relations currently provide an ADE RSOS/root-of-unity TL template; transport to percolation/Q4, and convergence to the intended Virasoro relation, remain new theorem work.
