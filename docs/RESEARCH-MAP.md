```text
measure covariance + finite confluent projector term + explicit field derivative.
```

Boundary normalization/prefactor terms are added where relevant.

## Research programs by scientific state

### A. Finite topological state: from one threshold to two activations

`main_integrated` digital Alexander duality identifies the finite matching charge with ambient homology rank. `branch_only` two-activation algebra then shows that a permutation has a first nontrivial-direction time `K1` and a second-direction-completion time `K2`.

The equal-mixture curve retains only the two marginals. The paired midpoint/gap distribution retains whether orientation dependence comes from:

- shifting the onset of any nontrivial winding;
- shifting completion of the second independent winding direction;
- covariance/cancellation between the two activations.

Because both activation tails have strictly positive beta-density derivatives on the physical interval, the finite matching curve is strictly increasing and its physical root is unique and simple. The decomposition therefore refines a well-defined root rather than selecting among multiple crossings.

The completed existing-data decomposition finds `K1` to be the larger linearized root-shift component at every archived size. `K2` reinforces at seven sizes and has an opposite-sign point estimate at N=265/325/425; these three negative rows are individually unresolved, so their shared interpretation is a next geometry/sector question rather than a claimed crossover. The decomposition is now the cheapest concrete bridge between exact topology, threshold-rank archives and the global H4 phenomenon.

### B. Continuum selection: ordinary endpoint versus charged access

The old question “H4 or H8?” is now subordinate to “which observer can couple to which representation?”

- The lower `V_(2,+/-2)` field is an exact four-leg `[2]` candidate.
- Under a regular unlabeled zero-leg endpoint hypothesis, the ordinary global linear coupling to `[2]` is zero.
- The Q=4 width-four transfer calculation confirms the matrix-element zero while retaining nonzero charged-block traces.
- Translation/reflection do not explain the zero: at width four, the spin-4 cosine and scalar share the same spatial sector.
- A transposition seam, charged insertion, singular Q residue or defect endpoint accesses different matrix elements and remains live.

The direct module discriminator is not another radial exponent fit: a calibrated thermal-Q4 positive-mode projection predicts `4:-6:3`, while an ordinary primary predicts zero.

### C. Low-dimensional state: failures and positive recurrences together

Three results must be read jointly:

1. N145->290 rejects one scalar full-curve multiplier.
2. Open PR #273 rejects analytic q2 on norm 4; scalar/common-generator Jordan survives at 5% but with lineage tension.
3. The annulus one-coordinate odd-shell model fails, while a two-state recurrence fixed by N325/N425 passes held-out N365.

The economical live object is therefore a small transfer/state model that may depend on context. A single generator across Gaussian cover and annulus is a hypothesis; a morphism-enriched state is the principal alternative.

### D. Geometry memory: endpoint versus filtration

An unmarked final Gaussian join is fixed by the CRT quotient and cannot remember factor order. A path-ordered exact oracle nevertheless finds nonzero activation-time and conditional-information rows from intermediate ranks.

Minimum retained state for a future ordered homology acquisition is per-colour `h0,h2,h5,h25`. A chronological version of the nonlocal interaction `R_c` needs richer dynamic lineage; scalar final ranks are insufficient. This is an acquisition design result, not a Jordan or continuum-memory claim.

### E. Logarithmic positive control and normalization

Open PR #246 now contains a completed cross-cutoff triangular production result, not merely ready code. The frozen model passes and the top-top cutoff shear is nonzero. The reported `kappa_proxy` lives in a connection-normalized field gauge and cannot be made universal by invoking the exponent alone.

The next statistic should close the gauge rather than repeat the same two-point shear: one top field plus two two-point-normalized macroscopic spin insertions yields a candidate invariant `CL` and a one-parameter shear gate. The same construction can then be transported to square-bond and square-site controls.

### F. Complex modulus and C3 character

The three degree-2 children of the hexagonal point distinguish three ordinary modular-ring shapes:

```text
E4:    (1,zeta,zeta^2)   DFT r=1
E6:    (1,1,1)           DFT r=0
E4^2:  (1,zeta^2,zeta)   DFT r=2
```

Real-only rows alias E4 and E4-squared. A common complex frame and an imaginary channel are essential. A multi-component DFT result is not a nuisance by default; it is direct evidence for mixing, a tangent, or a nonlocal sector.

### G. Q tangent: a compositional interface

The relative-source and projector work turns “differentiate in Q” into a typed interface:

1. the finite FK measure score;
2. the finite confluent part of a singular projector combination;
3. the explicit derivative of the bare field/insertion;
4. for boundary correlators, the conformal-prefactor derivative and amplitude-gauge projection.

The boundary CLE tangent supplies an exact function-valued control, while the tiny VJS collision supplies a finite algebraic control. The bulk matching observable should only be interpreted after one of these reproduces the full decomposition.

### H. Relative source: scalar closure and the next algebra

The matching charge takes values `{-1,0,1}` and satisfies `q^3=q`, so all higher scalar source rows reduce to `1,q,q^2`. The resulting algebra is isomorphic to three copies of the base field and has `HH^2=0`.

This does not eliminate a matching derivative defect. It locates it outside the scalar sector algebra—in connectivity-resolving junctions, a non-semisimple radical/module extension, singular projectors, doubled-space exchange, or marked/charged insertions.

### I. Threshold origin remains a separate program

Exact block-event semantics, a linear evaluator and a confidence-feasibility gate are now present on `main`. Earlier independent-bond gadget and finite-polynomial results also constrain simple mechanisms. None of these derives the square-site threshold.

Correlated hyperedges, self-dual closures, symbolic obstructions and block-event confidence constructions remain useful in parallel. Success or failure here does not upgrade or weaken operator identification by itself.
