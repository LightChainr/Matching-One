## 3. Long-lived tracks

### A. Exact matching, topology and semantics

Canonical assets include finite matching/complement identities, arbitrary integer-period torus topology, exact Gaussian quotient arithmetic, typed observable descriptors, and the exact C4 self-matching tangent.

The exact square-bond control now sharpens the duality convention: geometric dual transport, not bit-complement, swaps primal/dual wrapping and forces the odd center channel to vanish at `p=1/2`.

Useful continuations: Euler/Betti lifts, broader channel algebra, and large-N self-matching tangent decomposition.

### B. Threshold ranks as reusable finite state

`K_minus/K_plus` histograms reconstruct full finite matching curves; joint moments preserve information unavailable from the marginals alone. The archive supports roots, derivatives, reliability signatures, pivotal identities, Krawtchouk/Hermite response modes, paired rank-gap observables and low-rank transfer analysis.

### C. Central matching-odd Gaussian sector

The empirical signal is reproducible. Tested data are compatible with

```text
DeltaM ~ DeltaCos4 * N^-13/8.
```

Evidence includes same-N confirmation, held-out radial tests, norm-2 lineages, N=185/265 new geometry, root closure, and the completed norm-5 N=325/425 split-prime test.

Norm-5 gives

```text
H4:  0.4163 / 2

```

Thus the frozen H12/H8 aliases are strongly disfavored relative to H4. Because zero remains compatible with the child block, the result is a multiplier/harmonic discriminator rather than a standalone child-effect detection.

Open: unique asymptotic exponent, global H4 uniqueness, and continuum operator identification.

### D. Gaussian semigroup, root movement and thermal metric

P35/P45 show local root movement follows `-DeltaM/mean(M')`. P49 resolves a finite-size correction to the bare center-slope multiplier. The fixed-coordinate `145->290` lineage already passed prospectively.

Prospective N=185/265:

```text

P4[D]   ~ N^-13/8     survives
P4[D']  ~ N^-5/8      survives
```

P57 leaves q=2 versus Jordan unresolved. Two simpler post-reveal scalar mechanisms now fail:

The live direction is therefore a small multivariate transfer/mixing state, not another one-parameter width/boundary correction.

### F. Continuum/operator identification

The ordinary `c=0,h=5/8` module contains the non-null level-4 spin-4 candidate with bulk `x=21/4`; its declared torus one-point ratio has the exact weight-4 `g2/E4` fingerprint. Rectangular weight-4 ratios are also exact assets.

Independent bridges remain distinct:

- microscopic matching tangent -> RG blocks;
- Russo/pivotal -> orientation-resolved four-arm structure;
- FK/Potts topological sectors;
- torus modular fingerprints;
- Gaussian isogeny transfer laws.

Norm-5 makes the old H12/H8 aliases much less plausible, but it does not prove that the lattice observable uniquely couples to ordinary thermal Q4.

### G. CM/isogeny and shape spectroscopy

Gaussian multiplication gives exact finite covers. Pure eigenfields, finite mixing blocks and Jordan structures imply different composition laws. Torus modulus gives an orthogonal information axis.

Use this track after the held-out N290 transfer test to choose the next multiplier/modulus by expected model separation, not by size alone.

### H. Exact finite algebra/reliability

This track includes exact small matching polynomials, bounded complex-zero tests, C4 N=10, N=26 Beta falsification, reliability signatures and exact square-bond duality.

Negative exact results are first-class because they remove mechanism classes cheaply.

### I. Threshold value/literature

Published square-site estimates remain method-specific evidence with explicit provenance. Good next-width prediction does not guarantee an unbiased infinite-width intercept; Padé/rational alternatives did not automatically cure finite-width drift.

This remains independent of the orientation/operator critical path.
