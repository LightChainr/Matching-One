This stack must be integrated in dependency order or replaced by a clearly documented curated integration. It must not remain an indefinite alternate history.

### Known P0 defects or limitations

- #39: production executables were not identified at run time by a clean committed source SHA; clean replay is mandatory.
- #39/#46: the threshold-rank permutation stream couples sizes implicitly unless `N` is domain-separated; radial scores require measured full covariance under the retained policy.
- The repository currently has no active ruleset protecting `main`.
- CI is introduced by the governance baseline and must be required before future scientific integration.
- Published threshold data are not yet represented by a provenance-complete canonical manifest (#4).

These limitations are not a blanket retraction of the finite-size signs. They constrain the claim level and the next execution order.

## Explicit non-claims

The project currently does not claim:

- an exact decimal value beyond the cited numerical methods;
- a closed form for square-site `p_c`;
- proof of asymptotic exponent `13/8` or root exponent `2` in `N`;
- proof of an `x=21/4` LCFT identification;
- universality of a rational derivative invariant;
- a successful GPU production gate;
- a new rigorous percolation bound.

## Updating this ledger

A claim upgrade, downgrade, or retraction requires a pull request that links the exact evidence, identifies the old and new level, and states which alternative explanations were tested. Raw evidence should remain preserved even when the ledger changes.
