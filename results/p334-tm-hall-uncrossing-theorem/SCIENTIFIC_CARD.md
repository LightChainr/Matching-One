# Scientific card: the Hall family collapses

1. **Input.** Replicated TM is a Hall problem on weighted demand and supply pair graphs.
2. **Symmetry.** Torus translations make both pair graphs exactly regular for every fixed carrier, layer and projective line.
3. **Theorem.** `g(U)=|U|/N*g(V)+(D_cut(U)+S_cut(U))/2`; hence the all-site TM cut implies every proper Hall cut.
4. **Uncrossing.** `g` is submodular with slack `(D+S)(A\B,B\A)`; bisubmodularity and line-coset casework are unnecessary.
5. **Audit/frontier.** 2,470,440 cuts pass the identity; all-site is uniquely ratio-worst in all 688 positive rows. Only aggregate TM remains open in general.
