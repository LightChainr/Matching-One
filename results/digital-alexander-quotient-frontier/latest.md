# Short-period digital Alexander quotient frontier

Every permutation of every two-dimensional HNF quotient of index 2 through 7 is exhausted.

- HNF representatives: `40`;
- honest four-corner representatives: `17`;
- self-identifying face representatives: `23`;
- complete filtration paths: `49878`.

## Failure census

The face-corner classification is reported explicitly so a failure on a self-identifying
quotient is not confused with the already proved regular square-cell theorem.

## Boundary

The result is exhaustive only through index 7. It does not prove an unrestricted theorem
for all degenerate quotients or alter production data semantics.
