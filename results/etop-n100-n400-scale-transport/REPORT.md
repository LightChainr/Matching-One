# N100 to N400: an actual new scale, not a rescore

All periods are doubled. The three moduli and rational rotation are fixed, while the Smith pair changes uniformly from(1,100)/(5,20) to(2,200)/(10,40). The N400 seed/block is independent of N100.

## Does the coordinate-free E deformation keep its direction?

A freely signed amplitude gives N400/N100=-0.0302614189, chi-square=3.7917206/5, nominal p=0.579776. Both source and target covariance matrices are used. No area exponent is imposed.

Approximate 95% profile intervals for this amplitude: [[-0.20682181885119144, 0.18346544491457464]]. Zero amplitude costs only delta-chi-square=0.109138; the best-fit sign must not be described as a measured reversal.
