# Scientific card

1. **Exact transport identity:** `bias=-Var_k(h_x)/(1-xi_k)+Cov_(k+1)(h_beta,h_x)/(1-beta_(k+1))`.
2. **Conditional lemma:** edge slack dominating the variance penalty plus nonnegative aggregate birth/exit covariance implies uniform exit-hazard monotonicity and, on both carriers, ULC.
3. **Bounded strength:** both hypotheses pass 984/984 pairs; the maximum `(-bias)/slack` is `5/14`, attained by eight `N=12` matching realizations, with no nontrivial equality.
4. **Stronger routes fail:** first-order transport has an exact `N=12` tail counterexample; pointwise birth/exit comonotonicity fails at `N=8`; worst-case Cauchy fails 4/984 pairs.
5. **Open boundary:** the two aggregate mean inequalities remain unproved for arbitrary quotient size, so general ULC is not claimed.
