# P537 exact joint-incidence collar refinement

## Decision

The strict frozen-witness lift succeeds at the finite value level.  Among
`1,390` simultaneous 12-port sectors, `106` have all four pooled P4 cells and
`30` have all eight geometry-specific cells.  Every one of those complete
sectors has an exact determinant interval excluding zero.

The lexicographically first geometry-complete witness is sector
`075728b036142f7e2a9e`, with signature

```text
d=-2,0;arm=5;ports=64;j0=24766766658064;j1=142323964195344
```

and

\[
\det L_{075728}\in
\left[
-\frac{100138815227357659747831230667011}{25000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000},
-\frac{400555260909430638991324922668043}{100000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000}
\right].
\]

Its midpoint is `-4.005552609094306e-18`; the certified interval width is
`1e-50`.  The largest absolute determinant among the 30 geometry-complete
sectors is `+1.2009539184116512e-17` in sector
`7cfdd8e4e9cdbb317aa7`.

Thus the parent rank-two certificate is not solely an artefact of adding
incompatible source and thermal landing sectors.  Rank two survives after a
single common x+y+z component identity is frozen.

## Pairing contract

For each axial-distance-two source position `y`, enumeration is based on one
background with `x=y=z=0`.  The producer records, simultaneously:

- the ordered 12 terminal components
  `x[N,E,S,W], y[N,E,S,W], z[N,E,S,W]` at `z=0` and `z=1`;
- the eight source-port occupation bits, needed because an uncoloured
  singleton component does not reveal its colour;
- the base four-arm mask, terminal-incidence count and the four exact
  `z -> source-terminal` role masks;
- the relative source displacement in the fixed `x=West(z)` frame.

The same frozen identity labels the actual `y=0` ordinary-source packet and
the actual `y=1` source-absent packet.  The latter keeps its nonzero `q/E`
statistics and the `-beta b_z` Schur term while all a-fields are exactly zero.
This is a two-site counterfactual fibre, not a post-hoc pairing of the absent
sentinel.

All original filters remain fixed: `B_inf(z,1)`, `arm_mask=5`,
`J_B=J_W=1`, all corner words summed, `local_source_contact_mask=0`, rows
`0->1` and `1->2`, and columns axial2 absent/present.  The `y=(2,0)` slot
cannot form a complete sector because occupying it changes the collar arm
word; complete sectors use the other three relative axial2 slots.

## Exact coarse reconstruction

The legacy producer output is byte-identical to the committed PR544 input in
both geometries:

```text
axis raw SHA256   b3b4e2f3e17ebdf89b49b0e706cd709dfb21160f9ed3936075c4b1ab9670bff8
tilted raw SHA256 b3b85dafc254662bcce22aa6d27c7740ca3e9a6ba3d1ba916c872abd4622860d
```

Re-aggregation gives the committed 386-row CSV byte for byte, SHA256
`ff725ed0f222ce5dd5f2cc73cada4b93d618e21d73654df8ee5d2a3d7b319917`.
The joint sufficient statistics then sum exactly to every integer field in
that CSV.  After applying the unchanged root/Schur/P4 scorer, the sum of all
sector matrices reproduces the parent determinant interval exactly:

\[
\det\!\left(\sum_s L_s\right)=
2.6904188461441777\times10^{-14}
\]

with the same certified bounds as `results/p537-finite-collar/result.json`.

## What the coarse determinant measures

Determinants are not additive.  The exact decomposition is

\[
\det\!\left(\sum_sL_s\right)
=\sum_s\det(L_s)+\text{cross-sector terms}.
\]

Here

```text
sum of same-sector determinants   5.653307231999008e-17   (0.2101274%)
cross-sector terms                2.6847655389121788e-14  (99.7898726%)
coarse determinant                2.6904188461441777e-14
```

So the coarse amplitude is overwhelmingly generated by bilinear mixing
between sectors.  At the same time, all 30 admissible geometry-complete
rectangles are individually rank two.  The correct reading
is therefore two-part: the finite rank-two gate survives a frozen joint
identity, while the numerical size of the coarse determinant cannot be
assigned to one landing sector.

## Gate boundary

This completes the finite N25 **value-level** P4/Schur gate for the frozen
radius-one ordinary block.  It does not complete the thermal-jet independence
gate, a quantitative landing-margin theorem, cross-size persistence, or an
asymptotic four-arm transport theorem.

The present aggregate sums all diagonal corner words.  Therefore a separate
diagonal/corner-edge gate is not decidable from these artifacts; it would
require a predeclared corner-resolved refinement or a geometric separation
margin.  That missing statement must not be inferred from the nonzero value
minor.
