# Scientific card

1. **New exact class:** a boundary-regular two-carrier system is a complement-dual monotone rank system satisfying aggregate BA and TM moment inequalities on both carriers.
2. **New theorem:** BA moves upper edge measure to upper uniform measure; TM moves lower uniform to upper edge; together with duality they imply ULC.
3. **Minimality:** all 116 systems through `N=3` pass, but `N=4` has independent BA-needed and TM-needed exit-hazard counterexamples.
4. **Basic-axiom no-go:** monotonicity, complement duality, order-convexity and contiguous support alone admit an `N=4` ULC margin `-1/72`.
5. **Topological frontier:** BA and TM pass 984/984 existing torus pairs; proving these two integer moment inequalities from homology geometry is the remaining general problem.
