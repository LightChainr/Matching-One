# All-density face-kernel consequences after eliminating A

The proposed gain-labelled clique reduction is correct, provided that face
identities, parallel edges, port displacements and the occupied-face count
are retained. Its cycle correction is exactly `c(0)=0` and
`c(m)=(m-1)(m-2)/2` for `m>=1`. It works for the entire interpolation
`p_A=1-epsilon*(1-p)`, not just a small-hole expansion.

This is a corollary and quantitative extension of existing representations.
`3dc47674899a9ca93dc7d667f03c537fdb954bbf` already supplies the full
edge-midpoint hypergraph RC representation and twist readout;
`square-checkerboard-endpoint-homology.md` already proves lifted star
elimination at the saturated endpoint. The generic independent-bond gadget
no-go is also already in the repository. None is claimed again here.
The additional outputs are the exact A-elimination cycle/source bookkeeping,
a finite-density joint law for overlapping projected edges, and a closed
finite-source winding formula for the review's fixed B-row configuration.

## 1. Exact local kernel, including invisible faces

Use an honest parity-preserving square torus of N sites, with A and B
sublattices of size M=N/2. Fix the occupied subset `x` of B. For each A
site a, let `m_a` be the number of occupied B **ports** around it and
`z_a=1` if a is occupied. Write

```text
r_h = epsilon*(1-p),    alpha=1-r_h.
```

At t=0, conditional on x, the z_a are independent Bernoulli(alpha).
The local operation is:

- `z_a=0`: no connections through this face;
- `z_a=1`: join every pair of its m_a occupied ports, all controlled by
  this single switch, not by independent edge variables.

For ports with actual lifted displacements `d_i` from a, give the edge
`i -> j` displacement `d_j-d_i`. Keep its face identity and its port-pair
identity. Two equal quotient endpoint labels do not justify identifying
edges or discarding a winding self-loop. A crossing in a drawing creates
no vertex. Call the resulting B multigraph G_cl.

This kernel explicitly replaces the connectivity rule on B. At t=0 the
occupation marginal on B remains iid Bernoulli(p); that marginal alone
cannot represent the parent topology. At t≠0 the tilted face law below
is generally correlated, and the B occupation marginal need not stay iid.

Faces with m=0 or 1 have the same empty edge set whether z=0 or 1.
Their occupation marks therefore cannot simply be forgotten when retaining
K or S. They can instead be summed analytically, as described below.

## 2. Cycle correction and ambient winding: proof

Let n_0 count occupied A sites with no occupied B port, and let K_A and K_B
count occupied sites on each sublattice. Parent components containing a B
site correspond bijectively to G_cl components; its other components are
the n_0 isolated A sites. Consequently

```text
C_parent = C_cl + n_0,
V_parent = K_A+K_B,            V_cl = K_B,
edges_parent = sum_a z_a*m_a, edges_cl = sum_a z_a*binom(m_a,2).
```

Subtracting `beta=edges-vertices+components` gives exactly

```text
beta_cl-beta_parent
  = sum_a z_a [binom(m_a,2)-m_a+1-1{m_a=0}]
  = sum_a z_a c(m_a),

        m: 0  1  2  3  4
     c(m): 0  0  0  1  3.
```

The ambient integer winding subgroup is unchanged. Each clique edge
expands into its original two-edge path through a, with identical lifted
displacement. Conversely, every parent closed walk can be split into
B-to-A-to-B pieces and contracted into these labelled clique edges;
immediate backtracks may be discarded. Isolated A sites add no walk.
Both inclusions of winding subgroups follow, preserving the subgroup
itself, not merely its rank.

The extra cycle dimensions are local zero-gain relations. Abstractly,
mapping each K_m edge to its two-spoke star path has a cycle-space kernel
of dimension `binom(m,2)-(m-1)=c(m)`. It is generated by triangles,
whose three displacements telescope to zero. The face labels make these
local kernels independent. Retaining lifted incidences also makes this
argument valid for aliased graph quotients; the S identity below is used
only with the honest-cell counting assumptions of its existing proof.

Thus an uncorrected clique graph gives the right winding but the wrong
cycle count. Simplifying parallel edges is also unsafe for S: the review's
intact B-row example has five pairs of parallel edges, all with zero-gain
differences, which contribute to beta even though they add no rank.

## 3. The exact source-weighted kernel for every epsilon

With `q=rank(G_cl)-1` and the fixed identity
`S=2*beta_parent-3*K-q+2*N`, the transformed source is

```text
S(x,z) = 2*beta_cl(x,z)-q(x,z)-3*K_B+2*N
         - sum_a z_a [3+2*c(m_a)].
```

Therefore the unnormalized conditional face weight is exactly

```text
W_t(z|x) = exp(t*[2*beta_cl-q-3*K_B+2*N])
           product_a alpha^z_a (1-alpha)^(1-z_a)
                     exp(-t*z_a*[3+2*c(m_a)]).
```

Normalize this sum **for each x** to get the conditional face law. The
marginal B weight is its normalizer times the original B Bernoulli weight;
the q/E observer must still be averaged through the same kernel. All
weights are nonnegative and the finite partition is positive for real t
and interior p, including epsilon=1. No omitted-hole remainder is present.

If m<=1, the face does not affect beta_cl or q, so its z can be integrated
exactly with factor `(1-alpha)+alpha*exp(-3*t)`. For m>=2 the local odds
factor is `alpha/(1-alpha)*exp(-t*[3+2*c(m)])`, but the global
`exp(t*[2*beta_cl-q])` remains essential. These odds alone do not make
the tilted face variables independent. This is exact elimination of A
vertices, not a proof of finite-dimensional effective-coupling closure.

## 4. A finite-density joint prediction an independent projected-edge law loses

Consider two perpendicular neighbouring child side edges, each with
occupied B endpoints. Each side has two supporting A faces; the two sides
share exactly one such face. Conditional on B at t=0, label the three
independent hole indicators h0,h1,h2. The direct side-edge indicators are

```text
X = 1-h0*h1,    Y = 1-h0*h2,    P(hi=1)=r_h.
```

For every `0<=epsilon<=1`, exactly

```text
P(X=1) = P(Y=1) = 1-r_h^2,
P(X=1,Y=1) = 1-2*r_h^2+r_h^3,
Cov(X,Y) = r_h^3*(1-r_h).
```

An independent-edge model with these same one-edge marginals predicts
`(1-r_h^2)^2`, missing the strictly positive last term whenever
`0<r_h<1`. For p=1/2 at epsilon=1, the exact joint probability is 5/8,
the independent value is 9/16, and their difference is 1/16. This is a
specific finite joint-law constraint, not a new generic gadget theorem.
X and Y refer to the direct projected side connections, not reachability
through arbitrary exterior paths. Their correlation is derived
conditional on B, so it does not come from shared random B endpoints.

## 5. The two-hole review witness: a fixed finite-source cross ratio

Take the N50 periods `(5,5),(-5,5)`, B occupation
`{(1+i,i): i=0,...,4}`, and the two A sites `a=(2,0), b=(1,1)`.
All other A sites are held occupied for this four-state comparison.
Independent lifted-displacement BFS and direct `CB+CW+F4+Bvac` counting
give:

| A holes | K | rank | S | beta_parent | beta_cl |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| none | 30 | 1 | 22 | 6 | 6 |
| a | 29 | 1 | 23 | 5 | 5 |
| b | 29 | 1 | 23 | 5 | 5 |
| a,b | 28 | 0 | 25 | 4 | 4 |

Hence the mixed differences are `Delta_a Delta_b q=-1`,
`Delta_a Delta_b E=+1`, and `Delta_a Delta_b S=+1`.
After removing the common exp(22t), the four conditional weights are

```text
(1-r_h)^2,  r_h*(1-r_h)*exp(t),
r_h*(1-r_h)*exp(t),  r_h^2*exp(3*t).
```

Their normalized cross-product ratio is exactly exp(t). In particular,
with `0<r_h<1`, no independent two-face hole law can reproduce all four
probabilities at nonzero t. The conditional rank-zero probability is

```text
r_h^2*exp(3*t) /
[(1-r_h)^2+2*r_h*(1-r_h)*exp(t)+r_h^2*exp(3*t)].
```

This includes finite epsilon and finite source strength. It does not
claim that the witness supplies the whole second-order ensemble response.

## 6. An all-hole-density, finite-source winding formula on that fixed B row

Now free **all** A occupations on this same fixed B row, rather than
holding the other A sites occupied. The child row is a 5-cycle. Its five
sides each have two distinct supporting A faces; these ten faces are
disjoint between sides. The other fifteen A faces have m=0.

Let d_i=0,1,2 count occupied supporting faces of side i, and let D count
the sides with d_i=2. Write R=1 if all five d_i>0 and R=0 otherwise.
The clique multigraph has exactly

```text
rank = R,    beta_cl = D+R,
S = 86+R+2*D-3*sum_i d_i-3*n_isolated_occupied.
```

Set alpha=1-r_h and

```text
w0 = r_h^2,
w1 = 2*alpha*r_h*exp(-3*t),
w2 = alpha^2*exp(-4*t),
wplus=w1+w2,    W=w0+wplus.
```

Summing the local states algebraically gives the exact conditional
partition, up to its known constant factor,

```text
Z(B-row) = exp(86*t) * [r_h+alpha*exp(-3*t)]^15
           * [W^5+(exp(t)-1)*wplus^5].
```

Consequently

```text
P(rank=1 | B-row)
  = exp(t)*wplus^5 / [W^5+(exp(t)-1)*wplus^5],
P(rank=0 | B-row)=1-P(rank=1 | B-row),    P(rank=2 | B-row)=0.
```

The denominator can equivalently be written
`(W^5-wplus^5)+exp(t)*wplus^5`, visibly positive. At t=0 this reduces to
`P(rank=1 | B-row)=(1-r_h^2)^5`, an exact finite-density connection
prediction. The 15 isolated faces cancel in the conditional topology
probability, but are present in the partition and source. The global
exp(tR) factor cannot be silently absorbed into independent local odds.

This conditional law is not an evaluation of the original unconditional
two-geometry U. It supplies a concrete consistency condition any claimed
effective connection theory must meet, without a hole-density truncation.
