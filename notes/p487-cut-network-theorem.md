## Theorem and proof

Let an occupied induced graph A be honestly embedded in the oriented torus,
with ambient H1 image of rank one. Choose a simple essential occupied cycle
gamma. It exists because graph cycles are generated by simple cycles; at
least one must have nonzero ambient image. An embedded essential simple
curve on the torus is primitive and nonseparating.

Cut along gamma. This gives a cylinder with two occupied boundary copies.
Contract the old occupied connected components in the cut graph. Preserve
every vacant site as a distinct switchable vertex and retain its incident
edges. Let L and R be the distinct occupied boundary components.

**For every future vacant subset U,**

    r(A union U)=2  iff  L is connected to R with precisely U switched on.

All contracted occupied components are permanently present in this formula.
An L-to-R path, closed after regluing by an arc of gamma, has transverse
intersection number one with gamma. It therefore creates the second ambient
generator. Conversely, stack copies of the cut cylinder to make the infinite
cyclic cover transverse to gamma. Without an L-to-R connection, a lifted
occupied component cannot traverse successive boundary interfaces. Every
closed walk has zero transverse winding, so its image remains in the old
rank-one line. Contraction preserves these connectivity events.

The contracted network is planar. Cap both boundary circles, then contract
spanning trees of the occupied components and delete loops and duplicate
edges. These are planar graph-minor operations. We do **not** assert that L
and R are cofacial. The contracted network is a vertex-reliability object,
not a model with independently failing edges.

The argument is for genuinely embedded graphs. The implementation is narrower:
column-HNF square-NN tori with distinct nearest-neighbour darts. Rank-zero
checkpoints, abstract crossed matching graphs and unsupported degenerate
quotients are not silently included. Filling contractible occupied square
faces does not change the ambient image of the graph's H1.

## A coloring obtained before any future rank test

For a vacancy v, let C(v) be the adjacent old occupied component labels in the
cut network. A singleton trigger is exactly a vacancy touching both L and R.
Among the other sites, let S_L touch L, S_R touch R, and S_0 touch neither.
These three sets are disjoint.

A minimal pair {u,v} must have one endpoint in S_L and one in S_R. It triggers
exactly when these endpoints are directly adjacent or share an old occupied
component. Any shared component is neutral, meaning neither L nor R.

Consequently the trigger graph is bipartite. Its edges are the union of the
direct left-right vacancy edges and the complete bipartite edge sets

    S_L(C) x S_R(C)

for neutral old components C. Different bicliques may overlap, so their edge
counts cannot in general just be added. No Ferrers/nested-neighborhood theorem
follows. S_0 sites are isolated in the pair graph, but sites touching one side
can also be isolated.

The event graph is independent of gamma. A connected nontrivial component's
coloring is thus intrinsic up to exchange. Auxiliary assignments of isolated
vertices, and finer presentations by occupied components when a different
essential carrier is chosen, must not be mistaken for canonical physical
marks. The code freezes its cut from occupied geometry alone.

H4 orientation effect.

## Minimal triples are longer paths in the same network

Call vacancies adjacent in the contact relation when they share an old
occupied component or a direct vacancy edge. A genuine minimal triple has
one endpoint u in S_L, one middle v in S_0, and one endpoint w in S_R, with

    u~v, v~w, u not~w.

An L-to-R path using three sites supplies these conditions. If its middle
touched L or R, or u contacted w, a smaller trigger would exist. Conversely
the stated contacts connect the terminals and no pair subtrigger exists.

The resulting contact-network count is exactly 583 for A and 509 for B,
through 21 and 22 neutral middle sites. It agrees with the earlier full
three-site census. An independent potential-union-find checks that every one
of these 1,092 predicted triples really triggers; all proper pair subsets are
already covered by the exhaustive large pair check. No claim is made that
large-volume **nonpredicted** triples were all independently enumerated again.

## An update-closed state, not just a survival polynomial

The whole safe-insertion complex is the family of switchable subsets that
leave L and R disconnected. Its minimal nonfaces are minimal switchable
supports of terminal paths. The fixed-cardinality disconnection probabilities
are the complete unmarked survival vector.

Keep the initial cut fixed under future insertions. Activate a selected vacant
vertex, contract it with adjacent permanent components, and absorb if L and R
merge. There remains exactly one variable for each still-vacant original site.
Uniform choice among those variables reproduces the original permutation
continuation law. The rooted typed network therefore closes under updating
and preserves shared-prefix/fork experiments, unlike an arbitrary collection
of scalar reliability coefficients.

This is not a claim that recutting independently at each successor yields the
same graph-isomorphism representative. The fixed cut is a convenient gauge.
Nor is the network claimed to be the coarsest Markov quotient, to have bounded
size as N grows, or to preserve unretained geometric/charged local readouts.
The existing N16 equal-survival/different-fork example is explicitly preserved:
the network returns 95/196 and 93/196, with gap 1/98.

## Next scientific use

The new geometric result answers why the observed pair graph has two sides
and why its nonnested neighborhoods matter. The next saved-checkpoint analysis
can ask whether **component incidence and overlap** explain the remaining
population direction response. It must retain existing paired covariance and
must not count this exact explanation as a new H4 detection. A physically
interpreted component statistic needs a covariant cut rule or a demonstrated
cut-invariant definition.

For longer horizons, the target can be exact/controlled vertex reliability on
this network rather than a new scalar coefficient at each order. Treewidth or
separator methods are possible computational aids only after their actual cost
is measured. Perfect neutral components, switchable sites, and sampling
without replacement must remain explicit.

Literature interfaces (not proofs imported into square-site percolation):

- Duncan, Kahle, Schweinhart, arXiv:2011.11903: ambient-image homological percolation.
- Brown, Graves, Miller, Russell, Networks 76 (2020), 414-426, DOI 10.1002/net.21968: two-terminal node reliability, distinct from edge reliability.
- Goharshady, Mohammadi, arXiv:1712.09692: an exact small-treewidth edge-reliability algorithm; the vertex/fixed-cardinality adaptation is additional work.

No threshold bound, continuum limit, universal amplitude, CFT field or physical
Jordan identification follows from this contribution. Draft #267 is not merged
or modified; existing frozen claims and raw results are preserved.
