# Finite torus closure is a homology-decorated trace, not a regular colour endpoint

**Result.** The declared hypergraph law has an explicit finite torus
closure functional, including its original rank observables. That
functional does not factor through the invariant one-leg endpoint
`ell`. Its pair sector can already be nonzero on two parallel winding
clusters: the exact coefficient is
`Q^(c0-1/2) Q(Q-3)/2`, times the occupation activity. Conversely, the
specified S4 `[2,2]` seam projection has **identically zero unnormalized
q and E numerators**. These statements concern different contractions
and are compatible with the rational-family theorem `ell P_[2]=0`.

This note uses the source at `2690f665bc8029cb2370d3f1efcef5eb2853705c`,
[hypergraph RC and twist projection](closed-source-hypergraph-rc-twist-projection.md),
and the regular-endpoint result in the overview's
`notes/weak-q-paths-and-regular-selection.md` (`4409abec/e87d5de2/f0e9159f`).
The pair carrier is the one in
[`d006f9c1`](https://github.com/LightChainr/Matching-One/blob/d006f9c1/notes/p262-confluent-potts-projector-tomography.md);

## 1. A finite closure that retains the actual torus

Fix the honest embedded square quotient `Z²/Lambda`, a period basis
`(P1,P2)`, and lifts of its edge-midpoints and site-centres. There are
2N hypergraph edge-nodes; selecting an original site joins its four
incident edge-nodes through its centre. Every half-edge has a definite
integer deck displacement in this basis.

A transfer state consists of the partition of all edge-nodes, relative
lift potentials within each block, and the integral subgroup `H_b`
generated by its closed-walk gains. Potentials are only a gauge choice.
The subgroup is not replaced by its rank: its direction is needed below.
One may retain all edge-nodes rather than introduce a forgetting rule;
this is a finite mathematical transfer space, not a proposed large
solver. Let `J_x` perform the four-way gain-decorated join at site x.
For any fixed ordering of the N sites,

\[
 |\mathcal V(v)\rangle=\prod_x(1+vJ_x)|\mathrm{isolated}\rangle
 =\sum_{A\subseteq V}v^{|A|}|\mathcal C_A\rangle .
 \tag{1}
\]

Commutativity here means that the final gain-decorated partition is
independent of the activation order. It does not identify any dynamical
order observable. Write `c(A)` for its block count and
`r(A)=rank(sum_b H_b)`. Leaf removal and edge subdivision give exactly
the occupied NN ambient image, as in the source note.

For commuting permutations `(g1,g2)` of Q colours define

\[
 \operatorname{fix}_{g}(H)=
 \#\{c:g_1^{h_1}g_2^{h_2}c=c\quad\forall h\in H\}.
\]

For `O(r)=1,r-1,(r-1)^2`, the explicit closure is

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal L_{g,O}|\mathcal C_A\rangle
 =Q^{-r(A)/2}O(r(A))\prod_b\operatorname{fix}_{g}(H_b).}
 \tag{2}
\]

Indeed, one root colour in each block determines all its colours by
transport. It is admissible exactly when every closed gain fixes it.
Thus (2), applied to (1), is the torus numerator `Psi_g,O(Q,v)` with
its specified geometry, winding and observer. For identity permutations,

\[
 \Psi_{1,O}=\sum_A v^{|A|}Q^{c(A)-r(A)/2}O(r(A)).
 \tag{3}
\]

In particular, dividing the O=q or E numerator by `Psi_1,1` gives the
original closed-source expectations, with
`Q=m²`, `v=y Q^(3/2)` and the common partition prefactor
`Q^(1/2-N)`. There is no replacement of q/E by a marked observable in
(3). Noninteger Q is defined by (3), not by a nonexistent colour set.

For a one-seam permutation pi, set `(g1,g2)=(pi,id)`. If
`d_b Z` is the projection of `H_b` onto its first deck coordinate,
then its factor is `Fix(pi^d_b)`, with `d_b=0` giving Q. This explains
precisely why ambient rank alone cannot close a twisted transfer.

## 2. Why this is a trace and where the pair carrier enters

Cut the torus along the chosen primitive seam, retain all cut colour
ports, and glue the other direction. The equality constraints for a
fixed A give a kernel `T_A` from incoming to outgoing port colours.
Keep the gains through this gluing so that `r(A)` is the rank on the
original torus, independently of the colour permutation. Summing the
kernels with weights `v^|A| Q^(-r/2) O(r)` gives `T_O`. If there are b
ports and `rho_b(pi)` simultaneously permutes their colours, then

\[
 \Psi_{\pi,O}=\operatorname{Tr}
       [\rho_b(\pi)T_O].
 \tag{4}
\]

Equation (4) is a consequence of the explicit closure (2), not a generic
`tr P_[2]` substituted for the physical observable. In particular, its
rank weight is computed after the geometric seam identification, not
after discarding winding data. All equality kernels commute with global
colour relabelling. The finite integer-Q cut space consequently splits
as `direct_sum_lambda V_lambda tensor M_lambda`; a central projector
reads `dim(V_lambda) Tr(T_lambda on M_lambda)`.

By comparison, `ell` is an invariant vector-to-scalar endpoint. It has
only the trivial colour representation, hence `ell P_[2]=0`. A trace
can pair a nontrivial representation with its dual. There is no
equivariant factorization of that trace through a single invariant
endpoint; imposing one would change (2).

There is also a completely explicit pair contraction. Suppose A has
exactly two essential components, both winding `(1,0)`, and c0 other
hypergraph components. Each essential component supplies one colour
line across the seam. On these two lines use the equivariant embedding

\[
 i:\ |\{a,b\}\rangle\longmapsto
       (|a,b\rangle+|b,a\rangle)/\sqrt2,\qquad a\ne b.
\]

The diagonal-colour subspace and the antisymmetric pair subspace do not
contain `[Q-2,2]`. Therefore, for integer Q>=4, this component occurs
once and its contraction is exactly

\[
 \boxed{v^{|A|}Q^{c_0-1/2}\operatorname{tr}P_{[2]}
 =v^{|A|}Q^{c_0-1/2}\frac{Q(Q-3)}2.}
 \tag{5}
\]

A concrete original-lattice realization is the 5x5 torus with precisely
rows y=0 and y=2 occupied. Here `K=10`, `B=10`, `C_B=2`,
`B_vac=20`, `r=1`, and `c0=20`. Equation (5) is the exact coefficient
`v^10 Q^(39/2) Q(Q-3)/2`. No configurations were enumerated to obtain
it. The coefficient has a regular formal value at Q=1; it is not a
positive Q=1 sector probability and does not defeat the endpoint zero.

## 3. The entire direct-numerator colour spectrum is fixed

Let `Z_r` be the untwisted projected sum
`sum_{A:rank=r} v^K Q^(c-r/2)`. At any integer Q, rank-zero blocks
are insensitive to pi. A rank-two configuration has exactly one
rank-two component, whose saturated image is Z²; all other components
have zero image. Its colour count is therefore multiplied by
`Fix(pi)/Q`. Rank-one configurations have q=E=0. Consequently the
complete one-seam numerator class functions are

\[
 \boxed{\Psi_{\pi,q}=-Z_0+\frac{\operatorname{Fix}(\pi)}Q Z_2,
 \qquad
 \Psi_{\pi,E}=Z_0+\frac{\operatorname{Fix}(\pi)}Q Z_2.}
 \tag{6a}
\]

The point-permutation character is `Fix(pi)=1+chi_standard(pi)`.
Thus every other S_Q irrep has zero direct q/E numerator. The singlet
contractions are respectively `-Z0+Z2/Q` and `Z0+Z2/Q`; the standard
contraction is `(Q-1)Z2/Q` for both. This statement is stronger than a
single pair-sector zero, and still leaves the partition denominator's
other colour sectors intact. The literal representation assertion is
at integer Q; a real-Q sector completion must be stated separately.

### The explicit Q=4 pair projection

At Q=4 apply the fixed central `[2,2]` projection to (4):

\[
 \Psi_{[22],O}=\tfrac16\Psi_{id,O}
       +\tfrac12\Psi_{(12)(34),O}
       -\tfrac23\Psi_{(123),O}.
 \tag{6}
\]

For an honest embedded NN configuration, distinct noncontractible
components have a common primitive rank-one direction. If the total
rank is two, exactly one component has rank two; its image is Z² and
every other component has zero image. These are precisely the
saturation/disjointness hypotheses already used for the source's
annihilator formula.

For rank zero all seam factors are Q, so (6) vanishes. For rank two the
unique essential block contributes `Fix(pi)` and the others contribute
Q. Its coefficient is `4/6+0/2-2/3=0`. For rank one, let `(u,v)` be the
primitive winding, w>=1 the number of essential blocks, and c0 the
number of zero-image blocks. The colour part of (6) is

\[
 F_{22}(u,w,c_0)=4^{c_0}\left[
 \frac{4^w}{6}+\frac{(4\mathbf1_{2\mid u})^w}{2}
 -\frac{2(1+3\mathbf1_{3\mid u})^w}{3}\right].
 \tag{7}
\]

The remaining weight is `v^K/2`. Since q=E=0 at rank one,

\[
 \boxed{\Psi_{[22],q}\equiv\Psi_{[22],E}\equiv0.}
 \tag{8}
\]

This is configurationwise, for every activity and honest quotient;
all activity derivatives vanish too. The partition contraction
`Psi_[22],1` need not vanish. Any use of this specific seam sector in
normalized q/E must therefore retain the denominator. Equation (8)
does not assert that its contribution to a nonlinear normalized/root
functional vanishes.

The projected partition is signed, despite the original occupation
measure being positive. For u odd and divisible by 3, (7) is
`-4^(c0+w)/2`. This is physically realizable: on an LxL torus, L>=7,
occupy `x in {3y,3y+1,3y+2,3y+3} mod L` in every row. Adjacent rows
meet only at successive endpoints; the induced occupied graph is one
cycle of winding `(3,1)`. The negative coefficient is therefore not
an alias artifact. A central isotypic trace need not be positive when
its propagation operator is not proved positive. It is not a sector
probability, and its logarithm is not automatically defined.

## 4. The remaining intertwiner is now precise

The finite q/E torus interface is supplied by (1)-(4); the explicit
two-line pair contraction is (5). What is not supplied is an equality
between a **particular local four-leg field insertion** and either the
full central seam trace or the regular invariant endpoint.

To assert such an equality, one must specify geometrical incoming and
outgoing ports and equivariant maps `B: W_pair -> H_cut` and
`C: H_cut -> W_pair`, including their spectator contractions and
homology bookkeeping. The resulting matrix element is

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{W_{pair}}(P_{[2]}C T_O B)
 =\operatorname{Tr}_{H_{cut}}(T_O B P_{[2]}C).
 \tag{9}
\]

It equals the whole central trace only if the chosen maps resolve its
entire multiplicity space, namely `sum_nu B_nu P_[2] C_nu=Pi_[2]`.
One arbitrary pair of ports does not provide that identity. The source
score `2c_H-r+3K` is specified, but no equality between its insertion
and (9) follows from colour invariance. This is the exact missing
intertwiner for a local-field claim, not a missing definition of the
finite observable.

Thus regular one-endpoint Q activation remains excluded; the actual
torus permits a distinct, calculable trace contraction. Neither the
central seam projection nor its signed Q continuation by itself
identifies a continuum primary, a Jordan partner, or an additional
independent field.
