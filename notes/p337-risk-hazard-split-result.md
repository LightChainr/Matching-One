# P337 completion-current risk/hazard split

## Decision

The four-generation completion H4 is **conditional-hazard dominated at the
point-estimate level**, not generated by a changing risk-age composition.  In
all four primary blocks the conditional-hazard term points in the observed H4
direction, while the risk-composition term has the opposite sign and partially
cancels it.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| N85 | `+0.000722 +/- 0.001172` | `-0.001300 +/- 0.001284` | `+0.002022 +/- 0.002088` | 2.80 |
| N170 | `+0.001144 +/- 0.000278` | `-0.000385 +/- 0.000362` | `+0.001529 +/- 0.000542` | 1.34 |

| N680 | `+0.000156 +/- 0.000151` | `-0.000142 +/- 0.000164` | `+0.000297 +/- 0.000247` | 1.91 |

The table contains angular `p(1-p)`-scaled coordinates at the pooled matching
root of each block.  N170 is the only size where the conditional-hazard term is
individually resolved at the frozen `alpha=0.01` (`p=0.00607`).  The other
conditional-hazard p values are `0.345`, `0.101`, and `0.232`; generations were
not pooled to manufacture a stronger test.  Therefore the sign-coherent
four-size localization is the mechanism map, while the strict inferential
claim is the N170 localization.

## Exact attribution

For orientation `o`, primitive line `ell` and current layer `k`, define

```text
R_o(ell,k) = phi_(k+1)(p) P_o(K1 <= k < K2, ell)
h_o(ell,k) = P_o(K2 = k+1 | K1 <= k < K2, ell).
```

Then `J12_o=sum R_o h_o`, and the symmetric product identity is applied cell by
cell:

```text
Delta J12 = sum[hbar Delta R + Rbar Delta h].
```

The maximum reconstruction residual is `1.35e-16`.  If one orientation has no
risk support in a cell, its unidentifiable hazard is set equal to the observed
side, assigning the entire support entry to composition rather than inventing
a hazard contrast.  The absolute weighted mass affected is only `8.08e-7` of
`|Delta J12|` at N85, `3.71e-11` at N170, `4.56e-15` at N340 and `1.81e-25` at
N680.

Each size uses one aligned delete-one unit that removes the same batch from
both orientations.  The artifact stores the complete `3 x 3` covariance of
`(J12, composition, hazard)` per size.  N85/N170/N340/N680 remain four
independent dependency blocks, not four replicas of one fit.

## Age-standardized completion mark

The four primary archives do not contain `mark12_h4`, so this observer is
explicitly `not_scoreable` on the main lineage.  It cannot be reconstructed
from K1/K2/line counts.

The available marked controls were nevertheless exhausted without replacing
the main blocks.  Within each control, the next-completion signed hazard
`mark12_h4 * 1[K2=k0+1]` was standardized to the equal-orientation mixture of
the common `(primitive line, birth age)` risk distribution.

| control | age-standardized angular mark12 hazard | p | common-support coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P334 N325 2M | `-0.0000005 +/- 0.0003278` | 0.999 | 99.995%--99.997% |
| P334 N425 2M | `-0.0005591 +/- 0.0002330` | 0.0183 | 99.995%--99.995% |
| N130 20k | `-0.0003636 +/- 0.0020666` | 0.862 | 99.885%--99.927% |

None resolves at `alpha=0.01`.  N425 has a same-direction nominal tendency,
but its observed-versus-standardized risk-composition remainder is only
`+4.35e-6 +/- 7.44e-6`; the tendency is conditional, not an age-mix artifact.
These controls therefore do not identify the global completion H4 with the
local landing stencil.  P334 N325/N425 and N130 are separate lineages and are
not additional generation votes.

## Scientific boundary

This is a zero-new-sample reanalysis of immutable state-current and marked-age
archives.  It changes the leading mechanism from “completion H4 may be a
risk-age mixture” to “the observed direction lives in conditional completion
hazard and risk composition opposes it.”  It does not identify the missing
conditional state: unrecorded current geometry, cooperative branching and
other completion causes remain live.  It establishes no field, exponent,
intrinsic memory variable or continuum limit.
