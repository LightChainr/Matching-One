# Proof-carrying elimination and the Operator Passport

Issue #370 proposes a valuable change in question.  Instead of asking only
which local optimizer fits best, declare a bounded algebraic model class and
ask whether its full prediction image intersects a typed confidence set.  A
rigorous lower bound above the frozen threshold, or an independently verified
infeasibility identity, then excludes the declared class rather than one
initialization.

This should be an accelerator, not a permission system.  Matching One is an
interest-driven exploratory project: a candidate can remain worth studying
when a certificate is unavailable, an SDP relaxation is inconclusive, or only
one passport coordinate has been measured.  Priority records expected
information gain; it does not lock a task, require five approvals, or turn a
failed proof search into evidence against the physics.

## A useful six-coordinate passport

| Coordinate | Question | Smallest useful artifact |
|---|---|---|
| representation | Which Potts, deck, homology or defect projector sees the row, and which matrix elements are exactly zero? | One exact projector zero plus a charged/twisted positive control. |
| finite algebra | Which connectivity/TL/partition module and Gram radical carry it? | One explicit generator action, not only endpoint Gram data. |
| propagation | What word, path order and state dimension are required? | A covariance-aware multivariate Hankel lower bound and one held-out word. |
| sphere fusion | Does a normalization-free three-point ratio match a candidate family? | One frozen ratio with all field normalizations cancelled. |
| torus/modulus | Does a shape or twist ratio distinguish an ordinary, logarithmic or defect response? | One amplitude-free ratio on two moduli with a nonzero symmetry channel. |
| microscopic control | Does the same gauge-invariant statistic survive a second lattice realization? | One triangular/square or boundary/bulk positive-control pair. |

These coordinates are deliberately separable.  Completing one can change the
next target even when the other five are absent.  “Passport complete” is not a
status and should never be used to forbid work.

## Where proof-carrying elimination is genuinely strong

For a fixed state dimension, complex matrices can be split into real scalar
variables and their word predictions become polynomial.  The best first
targets are therefore small, already typed classes:

- scalar rank one versus an ordinary rank-two recurrence;
- shared-eigenpair commuting rank four/five on the frozen P250 bivariate
  archive;
- one shared square-zero nilpotent with the exact P333 Gram-compatibility
  condition;
- exact tiny-width projector zeros and connectivity relations.

A claim-bearing certificate needs all of the following: bounded or
Archimedean parameter support, complete coverage of the similarity gauge,
explicit treatment of singular covariance directions, an outer confidence
set with frozen calibration, and exact/interval reconstruction of the final
certificate.  A numerical SDP optimum by itself is a diagnostic, not a proof.
Dual weights may suggest which observations create the contradiction, but
their acquisition value is a project hypothesis rather than a theorem of the
SOS framework.

The P250 sequence also shows why certificates should follow, not replace,
model-free structure discovery.  Canonical Weyl and free shared-eigenpair rank
five fail, while the model-free degree-two Hankel score keeps separate
hand-specific rank-five charts compatible and rejects one raw shared chart.
The next short certificate is not a larger SOS ladder: jointly test each
candidate sector map against both hand-specific annihilation residuals.  A
direction-only singular-vector match is insufficient, and the archive still
lacks path order.

For rank one through three, the compiler should also be invariant-first.  Use
the shortest witness that avoids a latent similarity gauge:

1. exact semantic zeros and algebraic identities;
2. gauge-free Hankel minors and determinantal rank bounds;
3. trace/determinant invariant ideals and Cayley--Hamilton reductions;
4. bounded latent-matrix SOS only for the factorization classes that survive;
5. noncommutative or locality-bounded certificates only when ordered words or
   local tensor structure are actually observed.

This order is not a hierarchy of permission.  It is a cost hierarchy for
finding the smallest independently checkable contradiction.  The P250
bivariate archive now supplies the first statistical rank witness and a live
joint-null audit target, while the P333 signed connected-history construction
is already a non-artificial exact Jordan positive control.

## Literature-backed routes and exact boundaries

- [Pironio, Navascues and Acin (2009)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4368)
  establishes convergent moment/SOS lower bounds under boundedness conditions
  and optimizer extraction under a flat-rank condition.  It does not promise
  finite-order certificates for every Matching One instance.
- [Camia and Feng (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16047) constructs a
  triangular-lattice percolation energy/log-partner positive control with
  LCFT two- and three-point structure.  It does not identify the square-site
  matching field or a cross-lattice universal coupling.
- [Grans-Samuelsson et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05503) gives
  exact-formula evidence for normalization-free loop-model three-point ratios.
  It motivates a sphere-fusion coordinate; it does not already classify every
  Jordan, defect or Matching One observable.
- [Nivesvivat and Ribault (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24491) relates
  selected loop-model torus one-point functions to sphere four-point data and
  modular covariance.  Modulus ratios are candidate discriminators, not a
  proven fingerprint for the current lattice row.
- [Ikhlef and Morin-Duchesne (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15742)
  supplies an exact finite-lattice singular-vector template in critical ADE
  RSOS/root-of-unity TL models.  Transport to percolation/Q4 remains a new
  theorem problem.
- [Liu et al. (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19830) shows how a continuum
  Jordan block can emerge from diagonalizable finite-size matrices.  A common
  nilpotent across geometries is a stronger Matching One hypothesis, not a
  result of that paper.
- [Lee (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08720) gives a rank-adaptive
  thresholded Ho-Kalman estimator under controllable/observable noisy LTI
  assumptions.  A Matching One word-Hankel contract must be established
  before its finite-sample guarantees transfer.
- [Bérczi and Schwarcz (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23262) proves ULC
  for the intersection of two matroids and more generally two
  `M^natural`-concave contributions, while giving a three-partition-matroid
  counterexample.  This suggests trying to realize P334's complementary
  carriers as a genuine two-object intersection or nested-pair projection;
  it does not prove that the torus carrier families are matroids.
- [Brocchieri et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00172) identifies
  multi-letter weighted-automaton realization with noncommutative Hankel
  operators.  The constructive noncommutative approximation problem remains
  open, so it is conceptual support for ordered P250 words rather than a
  ready-made fitting algorithm.
- [Boyde, Hepworth and Patzt (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12247) proves
  every central Temperley-Lieb element is fixed by horizontal and vertical
  diagram reflections.  A reflection-odd selector therefore cannot come from
  a central element alone; P333 should retain an explicitly typed noncentral
  or history-sensitive action when direction selection matters.

## Working rule

Use the passport to expose missing orthogonal information and use certificates
to eliminate sharply declared classes.  Do not use either as a checklist that
slows speculative mathematics, exploratory reuse or a cheap mechanism-changing
pilot.
