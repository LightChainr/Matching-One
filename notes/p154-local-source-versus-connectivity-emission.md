# A local interaction source is not a long-range local occupation readout

The new [fixed-K interaction reanalysis](../results/p154-fixed-k-interaction/REPORT.md)
is a completed measurement on the existing Phase-E stream. Its two-size
directional `Cov(E_top,R)` is unresolved (`chi²=.8173/2`, nominal `p=.66455`).
This neither proves zero interaction response nor explains it entirely by the
occupation-only term. It also does not invalidate the earlier connectivity-B
pilot: nearby endpoints with a global connection predicate are a different
observable from a local occupation polynomial.

## An exact distinction for the next physical interface

Under the **unconditioned Bernoulli product measure at zero interaction**,
let `f` and `g` depend on disjoint finite sets of actual quotient vertices.
The corresponding sigma-algebras are independent, so

```text
E[f g] = E[f] E[g],   Cov(f,g)=0.
```

Consequently, merely replacing one finite occupation polynomial by another
cannot produce a separated-support connected two-point signal in that measure.
This is a finite probability statement, not a statement that an LCFT field
does not exist. Conditioning on K or topology, turning on an interaction,
overlapping quotient supports, and globally averaged observables require
separate treatment.

A local source still has a meaningful response against a global observable:
`d_lambda E_lambda[E_top]|0 = Cov(E_top,Q)` in the positive measure
`P_lambda ∝ P_p exp(lambda Q)`. The topological event depends on the whole
configuration, so disjoint-support independence does not apply. The exact
decomposition `Q=R+H(K)` separates this response into a fixed-K arrangement
part and the specified second thermal derivative; finite fixed-K
orthogonality is not zero renormalized thermal overlap.

This suggests a concrete distinction in future work: define the desired
**connectivity emission or source derivative** before claiming a non-contact
two-point energy signal. The positive P398 A/L readouts are functions of
past-frontier connectivity and supply one explicit bond-cylinder example.
They are not finite occupation-cylinder functions and are not thereby
identified with the square-site norm-4 residual. A proposed bridge must name
both microscopic models, the emitted observable and the response being
transported. This is an explanation of the objects, not another permission
condition or a prohibition on local interaction experiments.
