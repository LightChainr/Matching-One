# The x=4 correction: exponent evidence versus spin/operator identity

Status: terminology and theory-boundary correction.

## 1. What Feng--Deng--Blote actually establish

Feng, Deng and Blote (`arXiv:0901.1370`) analyze several two-dimensional percolation models and find finite-size corrections consistent with the second thermal scaling dimension

\[
X_{t2}=4,
\qquad y_i=2-X_{t2}=-2.
\]

For the scaled magnetic gap they use, schematically,

\[
X_h(p,L)=X_h+a(p-p_c)L^{2-X_t}
+(b+d\log L)L^{2-X_{t2}}+\cdots.
\]

They also report that the **power-law amplitude** at this exponent depends strongly on the orientation of the square lattice relative to the cylinder.  In their exactly solved square-bond comparison the nonlogarithmic `L^-2` amplitude changes between parallel and diagonal transfer directions, while the logarithmic amplitude is much less orientation-sensitive after accounting for the length-unit factor.

This is strong evidence that the dimension-4 correction space contains an orientation-sensitive component.

It is **not** an explicit operator identification.  The paper does not label the orientation-dependent term as a particular Virasoro spin-4 quasiprimary, and it simultaneously allows a logarithmic correction at the same radial exponent.

## 2. Why the distinction matters at c=0

At scaling dimension 4 several effects can coexist or mix in a `c=0` logarithmic theory:

- a scalar/thermal correction associated with the Coulomb-gas/Kac `X_t2=4` spectrum;
- nonzero-spin descendants compatible with square-lattice `C4` symmetry;
- logarithmic partners/mixing that generate `L^-2 log L` terms;
- observable-dependent analytic or geometric contributions with the same radial power.

A finite-size fit to `L^-2` alone cannot distinguish these.

Orientation dependence is more selective: after a same-shape angular projector, scalar terms cancel and only nonzero square-lattice harmonics survive.  Thus the repository's quantity

\[
P_4[S]=\frac{S(\theta_1)-S(\theta_2)}{\Delta\cos4\theta}
\]

specifically probes an H4 component of the dimension-4 correction space.

## 3. Current repository wording

The empirically supported finite-size statement should be

> the matching-even angular sector contains a correction compatible with `P4[S] ~ N^-1 = L^-2` over the tested sizes.

A useful working interpretation is

> an `x=4`, spin-4, matching-even irrelevant field contributes to `S`.

Do **not** silently strengthen this to

> `P4[S]` is the second thermal operator `X_t2=4`.

That final equality has not been derived.

## 4. Ising analogy: useful but not proof

Square-lattice Ising CFT provides a mature example in which nonzero-spin irrelevant operators are allowed by lattice symmetry and the spin-4 field `T^2+Tbar^2` produces orientation-dependent corrections.  This supports the general mechanism “square lattice -> spin-4 irrelevant perturbation.”

It does not determine the operator content, matching parity, or logarithmic embedding of percolation.  The `c=0` theory is qualitatively different.

## 5. How to separate the components experimentally

### Same-N angular projection

`P4[S]` eliminates orientation-independent scalar `x=4` contributions by construction.  This is the cleanest current estimator of the spin-4 component.

### Self-matching C4 control (#44)

A C4 self-matching site triangulation has an exactly vanishing central matching-odd sector but can retain ordinary H4 anisotropy.  A resolved `N^-1` angular term there would support an independent dimension-4 spin-4 correction without invoking the square-site matching residual.

### Thermal-parity derivative spectrum (#48)

If the H4 dimension-4 field is matching-even, the involution predicts the alternating derivative pattern

```text
P4[S]  ~ N^-1
P4[D'] ~ N^-5/8
```

independently of whether the field should be called `X_t2`, an identity descendant, or a logarithmic mixture.

### Log test

Fit no-log versus `N^-1(A+B log N)` only after the fixed-power held-out score.  A log term changes radial drift but does not by itself identify the underlying spin field.

## 6. Claim hierarchy

1. **Observed/predictive:** an H4-like matching-even `N^-1` finite-size sector exists over the current range.
2. **Mechanism hypothesis:** it is generated by a square-lattice spin-4 irrelevant field of dimension 4.
3. **Operator identification:** identity-family `T^2+Tbar^2`, second thermal `X_t2`, or a particular LCFT/logarithmic combination.

Only levels 1--2 are currently defensible.  Level 3 remains open.
