# D4 equivariance of terminal partitions

This certificate fixes the cyclic terminal order `(0,1,2,3)` and its eight
dihedral relabelings.  It records the induced D4 orbits and stabilizers on all
15 connectivity partitions.

Every one of the 1,800 partition-join covariance cases is exact.  A second
1,800-case check treats the two-port wiring as explicit data: relabeling both
states and every declared outer/interface terminal leaves the logical output
partition unchanged.  This separates physical connectivity from coordinate
names and prevents a silent port-order convention from entering composition.

The certificate does not choose noncrossing states or define planar duality.
It makes no self-duality, threshold, ranking, or bound claim.  Issue 13 remains
open.
