# Projective current as a natural-coordinate Ward object

For an exact line-orbit character amplitude

\[
A_O(p)=\sum_{S:\,r(S)=1,\,\ell(S)\in O}\chi_4(\ell(S))
p^{|S|}(1-p)^{N-|S|},
\]

the directed subset-boundary census gives

\[
\partial_p A_O=J_{O,\mathrm{birth}}-J_{O,\mathrm{exit}}.
\]

Passing to the Bernoulli natural coordinate
\(\eta=\log[p/(1-p)]\) yields the exact finite identity

\[
\partial_\eta A_O
=p(1-p)(J_{O,\mathrm{birth}}-J_{O,\mathrm{exit}}).
\]

The checked certificate verifies this as a full polynomial separately for the
axis and diagonal line orbits on the Gaussian N=13 and N=17 quotients.  It also
checks the induced eta jets through order six and the endpoint sum rule
\(\int_0^1(J_\mathrm{birth}-J_\mathrm{exit})\,dp=0\).

Two consequences are more useful than another bare-p fit:

1. signed orbit shares of the derivative are unchanged by every regular scalar
   thermal reparameterization;
2. a net-current zero is a stationary point of the finite character amplitude,
   while a total-current zero is a pole of signed orbit shares.

This turns the orbit-resolved zero diagram into an intrinsic finite-volume
object.  It does not identify the continuum field or assert that the N=13/N=17
zero locations have converged.
