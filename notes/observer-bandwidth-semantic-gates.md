production data.  The checked manifest separates three contracts: independent
Bernoulli resampling, occupied/empty swaps on a fixed-K slice, and a genuinely
Palm-conditioned process with its own declared reversible kernel.  Names and
numerical similarities never convert one contract into another.

transform, an adaptive mark without a derived conditional degree, a degenerate
quotient without its incidence polynomial, a slice endpoint without explicit
handling, and Palm data without its actual reversible kernel.  A Palm fixture
with such a kernel is admitted only as `custom_kernel_only`; it receives no
product-Walsh or Johnson conclusion.
