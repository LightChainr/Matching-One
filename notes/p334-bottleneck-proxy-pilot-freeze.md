# P334 bottleneck-proxy pilot freeze

The existing 50k rows cannot reconstruct carrier shape because no occupied
adjacency structure was retained.  This fresh 20k/size pilot therefore adds
only seven one-pass summaries: essential boundary edge cut, multi-contact
vacant sites and contact pairs, 2-core vertices/edges, articulation vertices,
and bridges.

These are current-state observables.  They require one linear traversal and do
not save the configuration.  In contrast, two independent deck-cycle lengths
are not defined in a rank-one ambient image without conditioning on a future
rank-two completion, so that candidate is deliberately excluded.

The main statistic is fixed in advance: add the fresh paired
`Mshape-M0` shift to the independent high-precision 2M production M0 slope and
sum their full orientation covariances.  A common absorption requires both
size-level joint p-values to be at least `0.01` and all four absolute slope
retentions to be at most `25%`.  H2 remains a near-definitional ceiling only.

Exact tiny gates cover boundary counts, iterative 2-core peeling, Tarjan
articulations/bridges, and the existing virtual H2 oracle.  N325/N425 use fresh
disjoint seed/counter domains, while the two orientations of each size share
the same permutation stream.
