## Retrieved PDFs (theorem-body quotes in the companion)

### DCT16
- H. Duminil-Copin, V. Tassion, *A new proof of the sharpness of the phase transition for Bernoulli percolation and the Ising model*.
- *Commun. Math. Phys.* **343** (2016) 725–745.
- arXiv:1502.03050v3 [math.PR], 21 Jan 2018. DOI 10.1007/s00220-015-2480-z.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.03050.pdf`.
- Quoted: Theorem 1.1 item 3 (p. 2); §1.2 “Site percolation” (p. 3); abstract exponential-decay sentence (p. 1).

### GL24
- G. R. Grimmett, Z. Li, *Percolation critical probabilities of matching lattice-pairs*.
- *Random Structures & Algorithms* **65** (2024) 832–. DOI 10.1002/rsa.21226.
- arXiv:2205.02734v3 [math.PR], 20 Feb 2024.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.02734.pdf`.
- Quoted: (1.1), (1.2), (1.3) and the Sykes–Essam “motivation” sentence, p. 2–3; amenability sentence p. 3.

### GL22
- G. R. Grimmett, Z. Li, *Hyperbolic site percolation*.
- arXiv:2203.00981v3 [math.PR], 11 Sep 2024. (Companion of GL24; proves (1.3).)
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.00981.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract; Theorem 1.1(a) p. 3, displayed (1.1); Remark 1.4 (Amenability) p. 4.

### DKS25
- P. Duncan, M. Kahle, B. Schweinhart, *Homological percolation on a torus: plaquettes and permutohedra*.
- arXiv:2011.11903v4 [math.PR], 29 Sep 2023. Published *Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré Probab. Statist.* (2025).
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.11903.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract p. 1; Theorems 1, 2, 3 p. 5–6; definition of `λ□(N)` displayed (1) p. 5–6; exponential-decay sentence under Thm 2; lattice identification p. 3–4 (plaquette = cubical / square bond in `d=2`; permutohedral = triangular site in `d=2`).

### FK96
- E. Friedgut, G. Kalai, *Every monotone graph property has a sharp threshold*.
- *Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.* **124** (1996), no. 10, 2993–3002.
- DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03732-X. (The identifier `S0002-9939-96-03515-2` is a different AMS article; do not use it.)
- PDF opened: `https://www.ams.org/journals/proc/1996-124-10/S0002-9939-96-03732-X/S0002-9939-96-03732-X.pdf` (315460 bytes, 10 pages, valid PDF).
- Quoted: Theorem p. 2993; Theorem 2.1 p. 2994. Window `q = p + c_1 log(1/2ε)/log n`.

### Fri99
- E. Friedgut, *Sharp thresholds of graph properties, and the k-SAT problem*, with an appendix by J. Bourgain.
- *J. Amer. Math. Soc.* **12** (1999), no. 4, 1017–1054. DOI 10.1090/S0894-0347-99-00305-7.
- PDF opened: journal/JAMS scan (extracted).
- Quoted: definition of sharp vs coarse, displayed (1.2) p. 1018; ER giant-component example `p=1/m`, `τ ∼ 1/m^{4/3}` (1.3).

### Hatami11
- H. Hatami, *A structure theorem for Boolean functions with small total influences*.
- arXiv:1008.1021v3 [math.CO], 12 Nov 2011.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.1021.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract (generalizes Friedgut; improves Bourgain appendix; does not shrink the window).

### LSW01
- G. F. Lawler, O. Schramm, W. Werner, *One-arm exponent for critical 2D percolation*.
- arXiv:math/0108211v1 [math.PR], 30 Aug 2001. (Acta version not used: the file retrieved as “LSW Acta” was math/0204277, a different LSW paper on SAW.)
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0108211.pdf`.
- Quoted: Theorem 1.1 p. 1, `P[0↔C_R] = R^{-5/48+o(1)}`, triangular site; “only for site percolation on the triangular lattice”, p. 2.

### SW01
- S. Smirnov, W. Werner, *Critical exponents for two-dimensional percolation*.
- arXiv:math/0109120v2 [math.PR], 17 Dec 2001.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0109120.pdf`.
- Quoted: Theorem 1 p. 3, `θ(p)=(p−1/2)^{5/36+o(1)}`, `ξ(p)=(p−1/2)^{-4/3+o(1)}`; the arm-event hypotheses `P[A^1_R]=R^{-5/48+o(1)}`, `P[A^2_R]=R^{-5/4+o(1)}`. Kesten 1987 is their [13].

### Smi09
- S. Smirnov, *Critical percolation in the plane. I. Conformal invariance and Cardy’s formula. II. Continuum scaling limit*.
- arXiv:0909.4499v1 [math.PR], 24 Sep 2009. (Old preprint of the 2001 result.)
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/0909.4499.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract (Cardy + conformal invariance of crossing probabilities, triangular site; scaling *limit*).

### Zeng13
- X. Zeng, *A Russo Seymour Welsh Theorem for critical site percolation on ℤ²*.
- arXiv:1309.2273v1 [math.PR], 9 Sep 2013. **Not subsequently published** (the PDF calls itself “the current dissertation”).
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.2273.pdf`.
- Quoted: Theorem 1.1 p. 1 (RSW for critical site percolation on `ℤ^2`); matching-pair setup Def. 2.1–2.2; Remark 2.5 (`p_c(ℤ^2)+p_c(ℤ^{2,*})=1` from Kesten’s book).

### KST23
- L. Köhler-Schindler, V. Tassion, *Crossing probabilities for planar percolation*.
- arXiv:2011.04618v1 [math.PR], 9 Nov 2020. Published *Duke Math. J.* **172** (2023), no. 4, 809–864.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.04618.pdf`.
- Quoted: Theorem 1 p. 2 (RSW homeomorphism for invariant positively associated *bond* percolation on `ℤ^2`); Comment 1 p. 2 (same proof applies to site percolation); historical paragraph p. 3 (Russo 1978, Seymour–Welsh 1978).
- A file `2303.00332` retrieved in an earlier pass is **not this paper** (it is an unrelated speaker-verification preprint). Do not cite 2303.00332 for RSW.

### GPS14
- C. Garban, G. Pete, O. Schramm, *Pivotal, cluster and interface measures for critical planar percolation*.
- arXiv:1008.1378v5 [math.PR], 13 Feb 2014.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.1378.pdf`.
- Quoted: p. 5, expected number of pivotals in a unit quad `≍_Q η^{-2} α_4^η(η,1) = η^{-3/4+o(1)}` on `ηT` (triangular); four-arm characterisation of a planar-quad pivotal.

### DC17
- H. Duminil-Copin, *Sixty years of percolation*.
- arXiv:1712.04651. (Proceedings / survey.)
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.04651.pdf`.
- Quoted: p. 2, Russo and Seymour–Welsh as the 1978 RSW input to Kesten’s `p_c(ℤ^2)=1/2`; p. 3, Menshikov / Aizenman–Barsky exponential decay; Burton–Keane uniqueness.

### CS11
- D. Chelkak, S. Smirnov, *Universality in the 2D Ising model and conformal invariance of fermionic observables*.
- arXiv:0910.2045v2 [math-ph], 16 May 2011.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/0910.2045.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract (conformal invariance of discrete holomorphic fermions for critical 2D Ising / FK-Ising on isoradial graphs).

### DT19
- H. Duminil-Copin, V. Tassion, *Renormalization of crossing probabilities in the planar random-cluster model*.
- arXiv:1901.08294, 25 Jan 2019.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.08294.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract, four alternatives for crossing probabilities, including “critical continuous: bounded away from 0 and 1 uniformly in the boundary conditions”.

### Tas16
- V. Tassion, *Crossing probabilities for Voronoi percolation*.
- arXiv:1410.6773v2 [math.PR], 30 Jul 2015.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.6773.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract (standard RSW for Voronoi; box-crossing property at criticality).

### DMT20
- H. Duminil-Copin, I. Manolescu, V. Tassion, *Scaling relations for two-dimensional random cluster and loop models*.
- arXiv:2011.15090.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.15090.pdf`.
- Quoted: Kesten’s relation `ν(2−ξ_4)=1` recorded as an input for `q=1`; four-arm exponent not computed for square bond.

### BS20
- O. Bobrowski, P. Skraba, *Homological percolation: the formation of giant k-cycles*.
- arXiv:2005.14011v1 [math.PR], 28 May 2020.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.14011.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract (thermodynamic-limit location of giant `k`-cycles on the flat torus; exponential tails).
- **Do not cite arXiv:1803.06637** for this: that id is an unrelated PDE paper (Soave–Terracini). DKS’s [BS20] is the experimental companion; 2005.14011 is the theorem paper.

### SS26
- B. Schweinhart, M. Shuman, *Voronoi percolation: topological stability and giant cycles*.
- arXiv:2601.00793v2 [math.PR], 10 Jun 2026.
- PDF opened: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.00793.pdf`.
- Quoted: abstract / p. 1, sharp phase transition for `i`-dimensional giant cycles on the `2i`-torus at `p=1/2`. Location, not a power window.

## Published-abstract only (PDF not retrieved)

### vdB81
- J. van den Berg, *Percolation theory on pairs of matching lattices*.
- *J. Math. Phys.* **22** (1981), no. 1, 152–157. DOI 10.1063/1.524747.
- AIP PDF: HTTP 403 this pass.
- Abstract quoted from the AIP landing page (companion note Q7): Sykes–Essam relation; **counterexample** to “all mosaics”; derivation for a **more restricted class** under finite mean cluster size below `p_c`.

### AB87
- M. Aizenman, D. J. Barsky, *Sharpness of the phase transition in percolation models*.
- *Commun. Math. Phys.* **108** (1987) 489–526. DOI 10.1007/BF01212322.
- Springer / Euclid PDFs this pass were HTML stubs (≃1–3 KB).
- Abstract quoted from Springer: equality of `p_H` and `p_T` for **all translation-invariant independent percolation models on homogeneous `d`-dimensional lattices**. Covers finite-range site percolation on `ℤ^2`.

### BK89
- R. M. Burton, M. Keane, *Density and uniqueness in percolation*.
- *Commun. Math. Phys.* **121** (1989) 501–505.
- PDF not retrieved. Cited by GL22 Remark 1.4 as uniqueness of the infinite cluster (amenable ⇒ `p_c = p_u`), and by Duminil-Copin 1712.04651 p. 3.

### BCKS01
- C. Borgs, J. T. Chayes, H. Kesten, J. Spencer, *The birth of the infinite cluster: finite-size scaling in percolation*.
- *Commun. Math. Phys.* **224** (2001) 153–204. DOI 10.1007/s002200100521.
- Full PDF not retrieved (Microsoft TR 403). arXiv ids `math/9811085`, `math/9912001`, `math-ph/0103012`, `math/9811087`, `math/9903038`, `math/9903039` are **different papers**.
- Published abstract: bond percolation in a **box** of side `n`; scaling window for sizes of largest clusters; observable `|C_max|`, not inverse-CDF of homological rank.

### Kes87
- H. Kesten, *Scaling relations for 2D-percolation*.
- *Commun. Math. Phys.* **109** (1987) 109–156.
- PDF not retrieved (Euclid/Springer stubs). Used as [13] by Smirnov–Werner; the arm-to-`θ,χ,ξ` relations in SW Thm 1 are this paper.

### Rus78 / SW78
- L. Russo, *A note on percolation*, *Z. Wahrsch. verw. Gebiete* **43** (1978) 39–48.
- P. D. Seymour, D. J. A. Welsh, *Percolation probabilities on the square lattice*, *Ann. Discrete Math.* **3** (1978) / *Z. Wahrsch.* **47** (1978) 215–221.
- PDFs not retrieved. Existence of square-bond RSW recorded by DC17 p. 2 and KST23 p. 3.

### Men86
- M. V. Menshikov, *Coincidence of critical points in percolation problems*.
- *Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR* **288** (1986) 1308–1311.
- PDF not retrieved. Named by DCT §1.2 jointly with AB87.

### DHN11
- H. Duminil-Copin, C. Hongler, P. Nolin, *Connection probabilities and RSW-type bounds for the FK Ising model*.
- arXiv:0912.4253. Cited by KST as the FK-Ising RSW.
- PDF downloaded; Type-3 fonts, body not quoteable this pass. Do not quote the garbled extract.

### SE64
- M. F. Sykes, J. W. Essam, *Exact critical percolation probabilities for site and bond problems*.
- *J. Math. Phys.* **5** (1964) 1117–1127. (GL24 ref. [22] / [16].)
- Not re-opened this pass; the “motivation for (1.1)” sentence is quoted from GL24 p. 2.
