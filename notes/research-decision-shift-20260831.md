## Two qualifications matter scientifically

First, three mutually exclusive mechanisms cannot be manufactured merely
by assigning convenient signs. A first-entry and a completion contribution
may coexist and cancel; unresolved U is not evidence for exact zero. We
need competing maps with the same source, time convention and original
observer before pretending that a new high-statistics U run selects among
them. The theory lane is extracting that finite map from the completed
temporal work, without a wider lag scan.

Second, a successful P334 normal-source intervention rejects the specified
complete two-score label closure. It does not by itself identify the source
of the unperturbed Matching-One anomaly. That distinction prevents a new
independent block from becoming just a more expensive existence test
advertised as complete field identification.
