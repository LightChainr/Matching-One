# Four-terminal partition-lattice automorphisms

The four-terminal partition lattice has six atoms, one for each terminal pair.
This certificate checks every one of the 720 atom permutations, extends each
candidate through joins, and retains it only when the resulting map is a
bijection preserving every join.

Exactly 24 candidates survive.  Their induced maps are exactly the maps from
the 24 permutations of the four terminal labels.  Thus the lattice encoding
has no hidden automorphisms beyond relabeling.  The five resulting state
orbits are the five integer-partition block types, with orbit-stabilizer
products checked exactly.

This result does not choose a cyclic embedding or a D4 subgroup, select
noncrossing states, or construct a planar dual.  Issue 13 remains open.
