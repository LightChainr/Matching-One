# Relative-source rank bound for higher-point spectroscopy

## Exact statement

The finite matching charge obeys

\[
q\in\{-1,0,1\},\qquad q^3=q.
\]

Therefore the algebra generated by the single global relative-topology source
is exactly

\[
\mathbb R[q]/(q^3-q),
\]

with basis \(1,q,q^2\).  For any observer \(W\)—local, thermal, spatial,
Ward-projected, charged, or multi-point—the complete source-resolved row is
fixed by only

\[
\mathbb E[W],\qquad \mathbb E[Wq],\qquad \mathbb E[Wq^2].
\]

The three sector-weighted observer means are reconstructed exactly as

\[
W_+=\frac{\mathbb E[Wq^2]+\mathbb E[Wq]}2,
\quad
W_-=\frac{\mathbb E[Wq^2]-\mathbb E[Wq]}2,
\quad
W_0=\mathbb E[W]-\mathbb E[Wq^2].
\]

Equivalently, these are the three primitive idempotents

\[
e_+=(q^2+q)/2,\qquad e_-=(q^2-q)/2,\qquad e_0=1-q^2.
\]

No fourth linear response direction can be produced by repeating this same
global source.

## Connected closure with an arbitrary observer

Let \(F(s,t)=\log\mathbb E[\exp(sq+tW)]\).  Differentiating the exact source
PDE \(G_{sss}=G_s\) once in \(t\) gives

\[
\kappa(W,q,q,q)
=\left(1-3\mathbb E[q^2]\right)\kappa(W,q)
-3\mathbb E[q]\,\kappa(W,q,q).
\]

Thus the third connected source response of every observer is also derived,
not independent.  The formula is basis-free and does not assume that \(W\)
is a thermal score.

## Consequence for Issue #252

The rank-3 second-energy/Jordan channel sought in higher-point response cannot
be identified merely by taking a third derivative with respect to the single
relative-\(Q\) topology source.  Such a row is algebraically forced by the
one- and two-source rows even on a finite lattice.

This is a source-order no-go, not an operator no-go.  An independent observer
\(W\) may genuinely couple to a rank-3 Virasoro module.  The required new
information must enter through \(W\), for example:

- a thermal Hessian or higher thermal derivative;
- two separated energy/four-arm insertions and their cross-ratio dependence;
- a charged or defect insertion;
- a Ward projection such as the Issue #252 level-2 or Q4 residue gates.

Once such a row exists, the relative source remains useful: it resolves that
row into the three topology sectors via \((W_-,W_0,W_+)\).  What it cannot do
is manufacture the missing operator by source repetition.
