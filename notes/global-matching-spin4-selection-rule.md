# Global matching spin-4: the exact selection rule and its boundary

The useful exact statement is narrower—and stronger—than “the four-leg state is absent.”

For integer `Q>=4`, the unordered-pair colour carrier decomposes as

`C[{a,b}] = [] + [1] + [2]`.

Let `ell` be the all-ones covector, representing an unlabeled `S_Q`-invariant endpoint. The exact projectors from #262 give

`ell P_[1] = ell P_[2] = 0`, while `ell P_[] = ell`.

Therefore a **regular one-insertion matrix element** from an ordinary unlabeled observable to the `[2]` four-leg sector vanishes. The global matching observable has no cluster-colour label and no defect endpoints; under that natural generic-`Q` continuation it is a singlet, zero-leg endpoint. This gives two independent gates against `V_(2,+/-2)` (`[2]`, four legs), while thermal `Q4 epsilon` (singlet, zero legs) passes both. Spin four alone does not distinguish them; representation and topology do.

This does not prove that `[2]` disappears from every torus scalar. The exact counterexample is its categorical trace,

`tr P_[2] = Q(Q-3)/2`,

whose analytic value at `Q=1` is `-1`. A torus trace may contain `[2]` intermediate states even though an invariant linear endpoint cannot create one. Explicit character insertions, defect/twisted traces, or singular `Q`-derivative collision residues also evade the regular endpoint rule. The #258/#262 derivative ledger matters precisely here: fixed-`Q` unmarked matching has no such projector derivative, whereas a designed VJS observable can have one.

The smallest positive control is `v=P_[2]e_{01}`. Its invariant one-point is exactly zero, but `v^T v>0`. Thus the charged sector is live and measurable; the unmarked linear endpoint is simply blind to it.

## One-shot experiment

At `Q=4`, generic `[2]` is the `S_4` irrep `[2,2]`. Compare the unnormalized orientation-resolved transfer/numerator amplitude with an identity seam and with one transposition colour seam. Exact pair-space characters give

- singlet thermal `Q4`: `chi((12))/dim = 1`;
- `V_(2,2)` in `[2,2]`: `chi((12))/dim = 0`.

This is a `1` versus `0` discriminator requiring no exponent fit. The unnormalized numerator is essential: a normalized expectation contains a seam-dependent partition-function denominator and would not obey this ratio unless that denominator is restored.

The interpretation layers are deliberately separate. Projector annihilation and the `S_4` characters are exact. Applying the null to global matching assumes a regular unlabeled zero-leg generic-`Q` endpoint. Dominance by thermal `Q4 epsilon` remains a conjecture until the seam experiment or another independent matrix-element probe is run.
