# N650 Gaussian CRT square: exact linear no-go and nonlinear replacement

This note resolves the algebraic part of Issue #200's proposed two-path
`N=650` acquisition.  It is an exact finite-group result, independent of a
scaling ansatz or Monte Carlo data.

## 1. The two intermediate lattices

Put (R=\mathbb Z[i]),

\[
 \alpha=1+i,\qquad \beta=2-i,\qquad \gamma=\alpha\beta=3+i.
\]

For either source period (z\in\{8+i,7+4i\}), define

\[
 L_0=zR,\quad L_2=z\alpha R,\quad L_5=z\beta R,
 \quad L_{10}=z\alpha\beta R.
\]

The two paths are

\[
 L_0\supset L_5\supset L_{10}
 \quad(65\to325\to650),
 \qquad
 L_0\supset L_2\supset L_{10}
 \quad(65\to130\to650).
\]

Their explicit periods are

| source | after (1+i) | after (2-i) | final (3+i) |
|---|---|---|---|
| (8+i) | (7+9i) | (17-6i) | (23+11i) |
| (7+4i) | (3+11i) | (18+i) | (17+19i) |

Multiplication by (a+bi) has integer matrix

\[
 M_{a+bi}=\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The relative Smith forms are

\[
 \operatorname{SNF}(M_\alpha)=(1,2),\quad
 \operatorname{SNF}(M_\beta)=(1,5),\quad
 \operatorname{SNF}(M_\gamma)=(1,10).
\]

All absolute source, intermediate and final periods above are primitive, so
their Smith forms are respectively `(1,65)`, `(1,130)`, `(1,325)` and
`(1,650)`.

## 2. The quotient square is CRT

The explicit Bezout certificate

\[
 1=i(2-i)-(1+i)^2
\]

proves ((\alpha)+(\beta)=R).  Therefore

\[
 L_2\cap L_5=L_{10},\qquad L_2+L_5=L_0,
\]

and

\[
 D:=L_0/L_{10}
 \simeq R/(\alpha\beta)
 \simeq R/(\alpha)\times R/(\beta)
 \simeq C_2\times C_5\simeq C_{10}.
\]

The kernel from the final torus to the `N325` intermediate is (C_2);
the kernel to the `N130` intermediate is (C_5).  Notice that this is a
relative deck statement.  It does not assert that the entire `N650` vertex
group splits as the source group times (D).

## 3. Exact no-go for a linear ordered mark

Write a source fiber as (D=C_2\times C_5).  For any scalar field (f) on a
final configuration's fiber, let

\[
 (E_2f)(a,b)=\frac12\sum_{u\in C_2}f(u,b),\qquad
 (E_5f)(a,b)=\frac15\sum_{v\in C_5}f(a,v).
\]

Finite Fubini gives, pointwise and without an ensemble average,

\[
 E_5E_2f=E_2E_5f=\frac1{10}\sum_{D}f.
\]

The same statement holds for every fiber character projector.  Characters
are indexed by ((r,s)\in\widehat C_2\times\widehat C_5); the two factor
projectors are commuting diagonal masks in this basis.  Hence

\[
 [P^{(2)}_r,P^{(5)}_s]=0.
\]

Thus any proposed

\[
 C_{\rm mark}=(E_5E_2-E_2E_5)f
\]

or its linear character analogue is **identically zero for every final
configuration**.  This is stronger than a `q2` or Jordan prediction.  A
nonzero estimate would diagnose a labeling/implementation asymmetry, not a
continuum morphism-memory direction.

## 4. Complete connectivity joins also commute

Let (\Pi(\omega)) be the full typed connectivity partition on a source
fiber, including whatever lift data are needed downstream.  Let (R_2,R_5)
be the equivalence relations generated by the two deck kernels and define

\[
 J_j\Pi=\Pi\vee R_j.
\]

Since join of equivalence relations is associative and commutative,

\[
 J_2J_5\Pi=J_5J_2\Pi=\Pi\vee R_2\vee R_5.
\]

The honest cover square therefore supplies no natural antisymmetric typed
homology commutator either.  Gaussian multiplication, pushforward on the
complete connectivity object, and the induced maps on ambient (H_1) are
functorial.

## 5. Minimal surviving interaction: a symmetric mixed join defect

Topology can still detect that both factors were activated, but the minimal
canonical quantity is the degree-two, symmetric Moebius defect

\[
 \Delta_{2,5}h(\Pi)=
 h(\Pi\vee R_2\vee R_5)-h(\Pi\vee R_2)
 -h(\Pi\vee R_5)+h(\Pi).
\]

Here (h) may be the transported black/white ambient-homology type, matching
charge (q), or another nonlinear topology readout.  The included ten-point
oracle uses the equivalence-rank witness

\[
 h(\Pi)=10-\#\{\text{blocks of }\Pi\}.
\]

For the discrete partition its four values are (0,5,8,9), so

\[
 \Delta_{2,5}h=-4\ne0,
\]

although the ordered joins themselves agree exactly.  This proves that a
mixed nonlinear interaction is algebraically available without inventing an
order commutator.

For the production experiment, the direct replacement for a linear
`C_mark` is therefore a same-flow estimate of (\Delta_{2,5}h), with the
full connectivity/lift state retained until all four terms are evaluated.
An antisymmetric nonzero mark is possible only after adding a declared
nonfunctorial operation between stages: truncating the connectivity state,
choosing representatives, pruning edges, or applying an orientation-sensitive
local coarse graining.  Such an operation is extra model structure, not a
consequence of the coprime Gaussian factors.
