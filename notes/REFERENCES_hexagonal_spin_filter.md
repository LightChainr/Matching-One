References used for the hexagonal modular-spin-filter conjecture:

- Paul Roux, Sylvain Ribault, Jesper Lykke Jacobsen, `Torus one-point functions in critical loop models`, arXiv:2604.24491 (2026). In particular eq. (5.11), the single-block Potts energy torus one-point `F=eta^(2 Delta_(1,2))`.
- E. M. Brehm, I. Runkel, `Lattice models from CFT on surfaces with holes I: Torus partition function via two lattice cells`, J. Phys. A 55 (2022) 235001. Section 4.2.3 gives torus descendant recursion; the `L_-4` primary one-point is proportional to the Weierstrass invariant `g2`.
- Classical modular-form fact: the weight-4 Eisenstein series E4 has its elliptic zero at the equianharmonic/hexagonal modulus. The symmetry-only spin-selection argument is independent of choosing E4 normalization.
