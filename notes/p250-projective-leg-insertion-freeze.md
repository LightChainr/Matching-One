# P250 operator replacement: projective-leg freeze

The R1--R4 scan closed the local annulus family.  The replacement is therefore
not another radius: it is a root-marked rank-one wrapping-cluster insertion.

For a root `x`, freeze

```text
L(x) = 1{x belongs to a black NN rank-one component}
     - 1{x belongs to a white matching rank-one component}.
```

This is a genuine mesoscopic leg row.  Two separated roots in the same
rank-one wrapping component receive an exact common contribution.  Evaluating
the five fiber roots and taking their Z5 DFT supplies the charged `O_r(x)` used
by the existing `G_r(d)`, `C113` and `C122` interface.

The companion line-character row weights each rank-one component by
`Re[(P ell)^4/|P ell|^4]`.  Integral saturation makes `ell` primitive and the
physical vector `P ell` makes the character independent of a period-basis
description.  It is not the primary candidate: signed angular averaging can
erase a propagating component, while the scalar leg row is the minimal direct
test of transport.

Before any random stream, the implementation must certify the deck-character
law and construct a primitive wrapping component whose mark propagates between
separated roots.  The random smoke is fixed at 2,000 replicas.  Promotion
requires all four charged pair denominators at two separations; phase scores
are outside the selection rule.
