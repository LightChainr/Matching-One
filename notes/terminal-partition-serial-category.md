# Typed serial composition of terminal partitions

Four-terminal states are typed as `(L0,L1,R0,R1)` morphisms.  Serial
composition identifies the ordered right port of one state with the ordered
left port of the next state.

All 3,375 triples associate exactly.  An independent explicit graph oracle
constructs each three-state word before restricting to the four surviving
terminals and agrees in every case.  The wire partition is the unique
two-sided identity; the product is deliberately noncommutative.  Bilinear
composition of exact rational probability measures also associates and
preserves unit mass.

This finite typed category is a composition primitive only.  It does not
search words, impose planarity, construct duality, or infer a critical point
or rigorous bound.  Issue 13 remains open.
