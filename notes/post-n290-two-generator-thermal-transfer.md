- on the same N145/N290 block `P4[D']` is essentially an exact normalized
  multiplier eigenchannel while `P4[S']` is not.

These facts suggest a state-space problem: the leading H4 field is robust, but
its finite-size thermal-response block is not scalar.

The Gaussian phase character is


| `1-i` | 2 | `-1` | pure real phase node |

This turns the Gaussian multiplier program into phase tomography rather than
only scalar child/parent ratios.
