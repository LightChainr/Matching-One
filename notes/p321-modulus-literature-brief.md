# P321 modulus-shape literature brief

**Status:** literature-backed design brief for Draft PR #267. It records what is established externally, what is a new conditional Matching-One prediction, and which observation separates the live mechanisms. It is not a field-identification result or production output.

## Executive conclusion

The literature supports three separate ingredients:

1. a finite topological graph-polynomial projector;
2. its infinite-cylinder Temperley--Lieb sector crossing;
3. modular recursion for torus one-point descendants.

It does **not** establish the Matching-One fixed-aspect formula

```text
C_width(rho)/C_width(infinity) = E4(i rho).
```

That formula is therefore a new, falsifiable conditional prediction. It requires a Virasoro-transparent homology projector, one ordinary thermal-Q4 block and no additive defect/contact or logarithmic modulus function.

## What the primary sources contribute

### Finite projector and cylinder endpoint

Jacobsen's periodic Temperley--Lieb construction writes the graph polynomial as a difference of two topological sectors and turns its semi-infinite-cylinder zero into an open/closed leading-eigenvalue crossing under Perron--Frobenius conditions. The square-site cylinder sequence is numerically described by `n^-4`, `n^-6`, ... corrections, but the paper explicitly does not derive the leading `n^-4` power from CFT. It supplies no fixed-`rho` `E4` amplitude theorem.

- [Jacobsen, arXiv:1507.03027](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.03027)

Arguin's torus FK analysis supports the homology-sector interpretation and shows that individual winding sectors are permuted by modular transformations. A global `P2-P0` projector can therefore hide sector mixing even when its scalar total is simple.

- [Arguin, arXiv:hep-th/0111193](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0111193)

### Modular recursion is conditional on the block content

Gaberdiel and Lang show that torus one-point descendants obey Zhu-type recursions and modular differential equations; a scalar full-modular weight-four space is generated by `E4`. This does not make an arbitrary projected lattice amplitude equal to `E4`: one must first establish the fusion gate, a one-dimensional block, the correct modular representation and absence of zero-mode or defect contamination.

- [Gaberdiel--Lang, arXiv:0810.0106](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0106)

Roux, Ribault and Jacobsen make the missing type information concrete in loop CFT: the same field can occur on several torus combinatorial maps, and the map supplies additional selection rules and amplitudes. Their logarithmic blocks also contain derivatives of ordinary conformal blocks rather than a constant rescaling. A typed seam is therefore an operator-tomography axis, not another replication of the ordinary modulus curve.

- [Roux--Ribault--Jacobsen, arXiv:2604.24491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24491)

### Why a Jordan alternative needs a second modulus function

Vasseur, Jacobsen and Saleur exhibit the percolation energy/four-leg collision and a logarithmic partner whose scale transformation adds `log L` times the ordinary field. Translated only as a structural inference for this project, a descaled finite-size amplitude can require

```text
A(L,rho) = a f(rho) + b [g(rho) + k log(L) f(rho)].
```

Their result is a local bulk two-point statement at dimension `5/4`, not proof that the level-four descendant couples to the global matching root.

- [Vasseur--Jacobsen--Saleur, arXiv:1206.2312](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2312)

Flohr and Gaberdiel give a structural LCFT warning: modular torus amplitudes may require an extra logarithmic solution such as `log(q)` times an ordinary modular object, and that solution is defined only up to character additions. Their example is at `c=-2`, not percolation, so it is a counterexample to automatic one-function closure rather than a Matching-One prediction.

- [Flohr--Gaberdiel, arXiv:hep-th/0509075](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0509075)

## Frozen two-stage discriminator

### Stage A: cheapest falsification

Use aligned square and `rho=16/9` roots at `N=144,576,1296`, common counter/batch coordinates, the declared equal-area conversion

```text
C_width(rho) = C_N(rho)/rho^2,
```

and fixed `N^-2+N^-3` corrections. Score the parameter-free ratio

```text
C_width(16/9)/C_width(1) = 0.689248108004...
```

with the complete cross-`rho` covariance. Failure rejects the transparent one-function ordinary-Q4 model. Agreement means only compatibility, not field identification.

### Stage B: distinguish one function from enrichment

If Stage A is compatible or only mildly tense, add `rho=4` rather than a nearby aspect ratio. The `E4` curve is already nearly flat from `16/9` to `4`, while a logarithmic solution carries an explicit long-cylinder `tau` lever. Fit all sizes and all three aspect ratios jointly; do not choose a second function after reveal.

In the same acquisition, retain resolved homology sectors and at least one typed seam/charge contrast. The third aspect ratio asks whether one modulus function suffices; the sector/seam row asks which topological module or combinatorial map carries it. Neither observation substitutes for the other.
