# The 76/24 split is a narrow counterflow window, not a constant law

The exact N13 and N17 coefficient tables determine the complete source/sink
curves without further enumeration. Every orbit-resolved birth and exit curve
is strictly positive in `0<p<1`; so are the character source and sink after
the exactly opposite diagonal character is incorporated. The nontrivial phase
structure arises only after subtracting exit from birth.

Each quotient has precisely one interior zero for the axis net flux, one for
the diagonal net flux, and one pole of the signed-share ratio:

| geometry | diagonal-net zero | axis-net zero | total-net zero/pole |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 3+2i, N13 | 0.559816490590113 | 0.600766413703139 | 0.604189609835120 |
| 4+i, N17 | 0.606810393541699 | 0.588553491659107 | 0.586722083251882 |

The orbit-zero ordering reverses across geometries, but the inherited
`p_ref=0.59274605079` lies between the pair in both. Exactly in that paired-zero
window, axis and diagonal have opposite incidence signs; because their `chi4`
values are also opposite, their character contributions reinforce. Outside
the paired-zero window, the orbit contributions cancel.

This explains both the apparent stability and its limit. At `p_ref`, axis
signed share is 0.75574 at N13 and 0.76484 at N17, yet its derivative is
`-28.31` and `+55.53`, respectively. The value is close at one declared point,
but the local shape reverses and steepens. There is no geometry-independent
76/24 constant.

The microscopic classification is **common activity with orbit-composition
counterflow**. At `p_ref`, each net is only 1.5--7.7% of its positive
birth-plus-exit activity. Axis composition in birth versus exit differs by
only 1--2 percentage points, with the sign of that skew reversing from N13 to
N17. Thus the observed derivative is a small timing/composition residual of
two large common-phase currents, not a birth-dominated source.

The next falsifiable prediction is now sharp: on the next independently chosen
two-orbit Gaussian quotient, the axis and diagonal net zeros should remain a
close pair, and the character contributions should reinforce only between
them. Absence of that paired-zero window falsifies this mechanism class even
if a single-point signed share happens to remain near 76/24.
