# Literature-guided mechanism frontier — 2026-08-30

This note records the literature routes that change an experiment, rather than
collecting adjacent citations.  All proposals below are hypotheses until the
lattice dictionary is exact-gated.

## Immediate design consequences

1. **Make the completion route a gauge-covariant character, not only a time.**
   The current F3 archive records `ell_birth` and `tau2`, but not a marked
   representative for the rank-two completion.  For an inclusion
   `L subset F3^2`, the nonzero quotient `F3^2/L` has one projective class;
   the apparent three complementary lines are lifts/sections, not three
   intrinsic subgroup states.  Since `PSL(2,F3) ~= A4` acts on the four
   projective lines and the stabilizer of one line is C3, a fresh path row may
   record an algorithmically marked complement only if it also retains raw
   winding, lift/section, basis/orientation, transporter and ambiguity data.
   Only an induced-A4 construction, conjugate pair or another gauge-invariant
   product should be promoted.

2. **Rotate source rank by physics, not linear algebra.**  The completed
   two-observer production leaves the current common-projective lane alive.
   A new column should therefore be a cross-boundary connectivity fugacity,
   winding/seam character or endpoint-conditioned Euler hazard.  A reversible
   rotation of `[J_Dperp,J_S]` cannot create source rank.

3. **Search for emerging, typed Jordan structure.**  A finite transfer matrix
   may remain diagonalizable while a Jordan cell emerges with width.  The
   useful positive control is a specified pair of sectors with a shrinking
   eigenvalue gap, positive-metric overlap approaching one, and a stable
   Gram-Schmidt generalized-vector coupling—not a literal finite-size Jordan
   block or a raw Q derivative alone.

4. **Turn the connectivity radical into a small exact object.**  Instead of
   adding more scalar `1,q,q^2` derivatives, compute a link-state Gram kernel,
   induction/restriction and central-element action for a small annulus/VJS
   system.  Split bulk-Q, left/right boundary, both-boundary connectivity and
   wrapping/twist tangents so the measure, projector and explicit operator
   derivative are all visible.

## Mechanism hypotheses

- **Completion-character hypothesis.**  K1 selects an A4 projective-line
  orbit; a section-aware representative of the K2 completion route may carry
  a stabilizer-C3 character after gauge covariance is exact-gated.  A
  nontrivial completion character could explain why the H coordinate
  reinforces at the crossing while its fixed charged-plane projection remains
  nonzero after H nearly cancels at larger p.  This is our inference, not a
  result of the cited papers.
- **Euler-hazard hypothesis.**  The external Euler response may couple mainly
  to the `rank 0 -> 1` or `rank 1 -> 2` hazard.  Conditioning the existing
  Euler row on K1/K2 would test this before a new production, provided the
  required cross-products were retained.
- **Cross-boundary source hypothesis.**  A both-boundary connectivity tangent
  is more likely than another scalar Q power to escape the rank-one null,
  because it is an independent connectivity parameter in the annulus theory.
- **Emerging-log-pair hypothesis.**  The triangular cutoff result may be a
  finite-size precursor of an emerging Jordan pair.  It should be connected to
  a typed sector collision and overlap flow before being compared with square
  H4/E6 data.

## Papers used

- Richard and Jacobsen, [Eigenvalue amplitudes of the Potts model on a torus](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0608055) — exact cyclic-character projectors for torus FK sectors.
- Jacobsen, Ribault and Saleur, [Spaces of states of the two-dimensional O(n) and Potts models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.14298) — twisted partition functions and representation-resolved state spaces.
- Sinha et al., [Lattice Realizations of Topological Defects in the critical (1+1)-d Three-State Potts Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19703) — local lattice defect construction and fusion template.
- Duncan, Kahle and Schweinhart, [Homological percolation on a torus: plaquettes and permutohedra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11903) — nonzero versus surjective ambient homology events, the clean theoretical analogue of K1/K2.
- Bobrowski and Skraba, [Homological Percolation and the Euler Characteristic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.10146) — empirical Euler/homology threshold relation motivating an endpoint-conditioned reuse.
- Grans-Samuelsson et al., [The action of the Virasoro algebra in the two-dimensional Potts and loop models at generic Q](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.11539) — typed generic-Q module diamonds and rank-two Jordan partners.
- Liu, Jacobsen and Saleur, [Emerging Jordan blocks in the two-dimensional Potts and loop models at generic Q](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19830) — the gap/overlap/generalized-vector finite-size diagnostic.
- Dubail, Jacobsen and Saleur, [Conformal two-boundary loop model on the annulus](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.2746) — independent both-boundary and wrapping fugacities.
- Morin-Duchesne and Saint-Aubin, [Jordan cells of periodic loop models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.5483) — defect-number and twist-resolved periodic sectors.
- Ridout and Saint-Aubin, [Standard Modules, Induction and the Temperley-Lieb Algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.4505) — Gram radicals, induced indecomposables and central-element action.

The Q=3 defect realization is a construction template, not a Q=1 field
assignment.  The homological-percolation theorems use different cell models,
and the Euler relation is empirical.  Each proposed bridge therefore starts
with a finite exact dictionary or a small positive control.

Two additional dictionaries must remain open.  Richard--Jacobsen's cyclic
group permutes noncontractible bridges, whereas the C3 used above is the
line-stabilizer inside A4; group isomorphism alone does not identify their
sectors.  Likewise, the positive state/loop metric used for an emerging-Jordan
eigenvector overlap is not the possibly degenerate invariant bilinear Gram form
whose kernel defines a Temperley--Lieb radical, and neither is a Monte Carlo
covariance matrix.
