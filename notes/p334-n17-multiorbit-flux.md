# N=17 repeats the two-orbit source/sink mechanism

The next modest Gaussian quotient generated by `4+i` passes the predeclared
orbit gate. Its rank-one states use the same axis/diagonal projective basis as
the exact `3+2i` N=13 certificate, and the two physical `chi4` values are exact
opposites:

```text
axis:     (161 + 240 i)/289
diagonal: -(161 + 240 i)/289.
```

The full Boolean lattice contains 131,072 states. Enumerating each directed
subset-boundary addition gives exactly 1,114,112 edges, rather than `17!`
paths. Both line orbits occur in rank-one states and carry nonzero source and
sink traffic:

| orbit | rank-one states | 0->line | line->2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| axis | 36,516 | 150,824 | 81,600 |
| diagonal | 2,380 | 16,218 | 9,418 |

There are also 8,823 direct `0->2` boundary edges. They have no projective
line and therefore remain outside the character-resolved source/sink pair.

For every lower subset size `k` and each orbit separately, the exact
Bernstein coefficient identity holds:

```text
J_birth(k)-J_exit(k) = (k+1) A_(k+1) - (N-k) A_k.
```

At the unchanged `p_ref=0.59274605079`, axis and diagonal contributions again
reinforce `dA4/dp`. The normalized axis share is 0.7557399 at N=13 and
0.7648450 at N=17; the diagonal share is the complement. Thus the new
quotient changes the physical character phase but moves the source/sink
partition by only 0.91 percentage points.

This is evidence for a transported projective-current mechanism rather than
an N=13-only cancellation accident. It is not an asymptotic share estimate or
a continuum field identification. A third exact quotient should be chosen for
maximal change in the orbit incidence geometry, not merely the next norm.
