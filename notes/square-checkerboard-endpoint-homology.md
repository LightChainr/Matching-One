# Fully occupied square sublattice: exact finite homology endpoint

**Result.** On a parity-preserving square torus, filling one checkerboard
sublattice and retaining any occupied subset of the other preserves its
entire ambient integer winding subgroup under star elimination to a
matching-square child torus of half the area. Complementing the child gives
the ordinary square-site q/E endpoint. Isolated filled-sublattice sites do
not affect this homology, but they add a precise plaquette term to the
absolute cluster fugacity.

The connectivity/threshold endpoint is known: Wattendorff–Wessel,
[*Sublattice-selective percolation on bipartite planar lattices*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12821v2),
Phys. Rev. E109,044108(2024), introduction, explicitly relates the fully
occupied-sublattice endpoint to square NN+NNN percolation and the complementary
square threshold. The arXiv skill was used only to retrieve this specified
source. The contribution here is the finite gain/homology and fugacity
crosswalk, not a new model or threshold. Repository base4bb851cb already has
[digital Alexander duality](digital-alexander-duality-proof.md); its
[8/4-degree self-matching triangulation](c4-self-matching-tangent-improvement.md)
is a different graph from the degree4 square graph used here.

## 1. Exact lattice and period map

Identify the square lattice with Z[i]. Require
`Λ⊂(1+i)Z[i]`: every period preserves x+y parity. Even area alone is not
sufficient. Let A be even sites, B odd sites, all A occupied, and O⊂B the
occupied random sites. Put

```text
Λ' = Λ/(1+i),       M=[Z[i]:Λ']=N/2,
f(w)=(w−1)/(1+i),  W=f(O)⊂Z[i]/Λ',   U=(Z[i]/Λ')\W.
```

The affine map f extends to a homeomorphism of the two ambient tori, scaling
length by1/sqrt(2) and rotating by−pi/4. For every period vector,

```text
(x,y) → ((x+y)/2,(y−x)/2).
```

For Gaussian periods generated by a+ib, the child generator is
`(a+b)/2+i(b−a)/2`; a,b must have equal parity. A cycle with parent deck
address `(n1,n2)` retains that integer address in the pushed-forward period
basis. Subsequent HNF/unit/reflection canonicalization must transform that
basis explicitly.

| Parent positive Gaussian generators, in listed order | Child generators in the same order | Child area |
|---|---|---:|
| 11+3i, 9+7i | 7−4i, 8−i |65|
| 13+i, 11+7i | 7−6i, 9−2i |85|
| 16+2i, 14+8i | 9−7i, 11−3i |130|
| 18+4i, 14+12i | 11−7i, 13−i |170|

These examples reverse the familiar positive child ordering after reflection;
the listed direct map, rather than an assumed same-order identification,
fixes the geometry convention. No normalized-U interpretation is needed for
the homology statement.

## 2. Star elimination preserves gains, not the abstract cycle space

The four B-neighbors of a filled A-site b map to the four corners of one child
unit square: relative to b/(1+i), they are `0,i,−1+i,−1`.
Connecting each pair of occupied corners therefore produces the child
NN+NNN matching graph on W. Equivalently, each parent two-edge path
`w→b→w'` becomes a child matching edge (or a zero-displacement backtrack).

To prove the homology claim, perform the replacement **in the universal
cover**, not just on quotient vertex labels. Every parent occupied cycle
alternates between A and O, so all its two-edge pieces can be replaced.
Every child matching edge conversely expands through a filled A-site:
its displacement multiplied by1+i is either an axial length2 step or an
original diagonal step, each admitting such a two-edge path. Both
replacements preserve lifted endpoint displacement. The two possible
expansions of an axial child edge differ by a contractible original unit
square. Closed walks thus realize exactly the same deck translations:

```text
f_* im[H1(parent NN(A∪O);Z) → H1(T_Λ;Z)]
    = im[H1(child matching(W);Z) → H1(T_Λ';Z)].
```

This preserves rank, winding line and integer saturation in corresponding
bases. It is not an equality of graph Betti numbers or a literal contraction
of all neighbors to one vertex: replacing one four-leaf star by K4 creates
local cycles, all with zero ambient gain. For an embedded digital reduction,
remove a matching diagonal when an occupied boundary path in the same lifted
unit face replaces it; keep the diagonal for exactly opposite occupied
corners. Local polygon fillings may be used, but filling arbitrary cliques
of the **quotient** is not justified: their edges may carry nonzero gains.

Isolated A-sites have no edges and contribute no ambient H1. They are the
only occupied parent components not represented by a child W-component.
The original vacant matching graph lies entirely in B\O; its diagonal
steps map exactly to child NN steps. Therefore its gain graph is directly
isomorphic to `child NN(U)`.

## 3. Alias scope and the complementary rank identity

A convenient sufficient **nondegenerate implementation bound** is
`min_{0≠λ'∈Λ'} ||λ'||_infinity > 2`. It makes the complete child3×3 neighbor
stencil injective, hence all unit faces embed and all eight matching neighbors
are distinct. For a Gaussian child of area M, every nonzero period has
Euclidean length at least sqrt(M), so M>8 (parent N>16) suffices. All four
listed endpoint pairs satisfy this bound by a wide margin. It is sufficient,
not a necessary restriction on the theorem.

The path proof holds for every parity-preserving Λ when graphs are quotients
of the **lifted lattice edge multiset**: retain distinct edge gains and
nonzero-gain self-loops. A loop-free/simple quotient is outside this theorem.
The minimum warning is N=2, Λ=(1+i)Z[i], with its one A and one B site both
occupied. The parent has rank2 through parallel NN edges of different gains;
the lifted paths `0→1→1+i` and `0→1→1−i` give independent deck periods.
The child is one site with winding loops. Discarding these loops makes the
child rank0 despite unchanged ordinary connectivity.

For honest embedded unit cells, digital Alexander duality gives
`r_match(W)+r_NN(U)=2`. This rank identity also descends to aliased gain
quotients by a finite-cover argument: choose m so the periods mΛ' give
honest cells and periodically lift the configuration. Projection of a cover
cycle gives a base cycle, while repeating any base cycle m times gives a
closed lift. Thus, for either NN or matching graph, the projected cover
homology image lies in the base image and contains m times the base image.
Their rational ranks coincide. Apply the honest-cell identity upstairs and
descend. This covers alias rank observables under the stated gain convention;
it does not assert equality of local cell counts or graph Betti numbers.

Writing r for the parent occupied rank and r' for child NN(U), we obtain
configuration by configuration

```text
r=2−r',      q_parent=r−1=−q_child(U),      E_parent=q_parent²=E_child(U).
```

## 4. Probability and fugacity dictionaries

At independent endpoint occupations `p_A=1,p_B=p`, U is an ordinary child
NN site configuration with parameter `1−p`. Hence the complete finite
rank profiles, with F1=P(rank≥1), F2=P(rank=2), obey

```text
P_parent,r(1,p) = P_child,2−r(1−p),
M_parent(1,p) = −M_child(1−p),    E_parent(1,p)=E_child(1−p),
F1_parent(1,p)=1−F2_child(1−p),  F2_parent(1,p)=1−F1_child(1−p).
```

There are M random sites, not N: these endpoint profiles use Bin(M,p).
For the family `p_A=s+(1−s)p,p_B=p`, varying p at s=1 changes only B.
These formulas do not turn that derivative into simultaneous variation of
both sublattices.

Occupation odds y=p/(1−p) become child odds1/y. For unnormalized rank-sector
occupation polynomials this is exactly
`Z_parent,r^endpoint(y)=y^M Z_child,2−r(1/y)`.
Topological weights `exp(t q+h E)` map to `exp(−t q_child+h E_child)`.

The bulk **absolute cluster source** needs one extra local term. Let F4(U)
count child unit-face orbits whose four lifted corners are occupied in U
(use face incidences even when corners alias). Such a face corresponds
exactly to an isolated filled parent A-site. The component bijections give

```text
CB_parent = C_matching(W) + F4(U),
CW_parent = C_NN(U),
s_parent=CB_parent+CW_parent = s_child(U)+F4(U).
```

Thus `Q^s_parent` maps to `Q^[s_child+F4]`, **not** to the unmodified child
absolute-cluster fugacity. The Bernoulli q/E map above is exact at Q=1;
extending the source family away from Q=1 requires this plaquette weight.
This isolated-site correction is compatible with exact ambient-rank
preservation because component number and ambient H1 image are different
statistics. No fresh outcomes, simulation, enumeration or test framework
was read or generated for this derivation.
