With `u=beta^2`, `Q=4 cos^2(pi u)`, and the dense/FK percolation point `u=2/3`, the chain rule gives

`du/dQ = sqrt(3)/(6 pi)`.

The exact generic-loop formula then yields

- `V_(2,±2)`: `x=17/4`, `dx/dQ=-5 sqrt(3)/(16 pi)`;
- `Q4 epsilon`: `x=21/4`, `dx/dQ=-9 sqrt(3)/(16 pi)`.

Their dimension gap is `-1`, while the velocity gap `x'_22-x'_Q4` is `sqrt(3)/(4 pi)`. The latter is amplitude-free once a lattice observable has been promoted to a consistent generic-Q field family including its explicit projector/normalization derivative.
