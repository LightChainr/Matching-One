# Hochschild rigidity of the three-sector relative source

## Result

The finite matching charge takes only the values

\[
q\in\{-1,0,1\},\qquad q^3=q,
\]

so its scalar source algebra is

\[
A=\mathbb Q[q]/(q^3-q)\cong\mathbb Q\times\mathbb Q\times\mathbb Q.
\]

The isomorphism is evaluation at the three sectors.  In the primitive basis
`e_minus,e_zero,e_plus`, multiplication is `e_i e_j=delta_ij e_i`.  Thus `A`
is finite separable and semisimple.

The included rational Hochschild complex computes

```text
dim C1 = 9,  dim C2 = 27,  dim C3 = 81,
rank(delta1) = 9,
rank(delta2) = 18,
dim Z2 = dim B2 = 9,
HH2(A,A) = 0.
```

It also checks `delta2 delta1=0` and contracts every computed 2-cocycle basis
vector explicitly with

\[
h(\phi)(a)=\sum_i e_i\phi(e_i,a),\qquad \delta h(\phi)=\phi.
\]

Hence every first-order associative change of the multiplication table of
this three-sector algebra is a basis artifact.

## Consequence for the matching derivative defect

Issue #233 proposed that a nontrivial `Q -> 1` matching tangent might be an
infinitesimal deformation of a defect algebra.  That idea remains viable only
after the algebra is enlarged.  The scalar sector algebra generated by the
single relative source is too rigid to carry the anomaly.

The nontrivial object must use information absent from `A`, for example:

- connectivity-resolving junction or partition states;
- a non-semisimple radical or module extension;
- a singular `Q`-dependent projector;
- the doubled-space exchange intertwiner `Ad_J`;
- charged, spatial, or marked defect insertions.

This is compatible with the existing exact doubled-space result: the
matching-odd operator `D` can be a genuine pull-through obstruction under
`Ad_J` while still being cohomologically trivial as a deformation of the
three scalar sector idempotents.

## Stronger standard form

For a finite separable algebra in characteristic zero,

\[
HH^n(A,M)=0\qquad(n>0)
\]

for every `A`-bimodule `M`.  Consequently, merely changing coefficients or
attaching another ordinary bimodule to the same semisimple three-sector
algebra will not produce the desired tangent class.  The next exact target
should quotient out this separable block and compute the cohomology or
extension data of the remaining connectivity/defect radical.

## Evidence boundary

The finite-dimensional rational calculation and the vanishing of `HH2(A,A)`
are exact.  The identification of the larger non-semisimple algebra with a
continuum logarithmic defect remains a mechanism proposal.
