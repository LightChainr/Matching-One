# D4 orbit compression is not an exact serial quotient

The 15 labelled terminal partitions form seven D4 orbits, but those seven
orbit labels do not retain the ordered-port information required by serial
composition.  Of the 49 orbit pairs, 26 produce more than one output orbit
when their labelled representatives are varied.  The smallest counterexample
has only two labelled input pairs and already produces two different outputs.

Uniformly averaging the labelled outputs gives a normalized 7-by-7 stochastic
kernel, but that repair is not exact: 166 of the 343 orbit-basis triples fail
associativity.  Thus a composition search must retain a port frame, a labelled
state, or equivalent orientation-resolved information between steps.

This is an information-boundary result, not a choice of gauge or a planar-dual
construction.  It does not search composition words or claim a threshold or
bound.  Issue 13 remains open.
