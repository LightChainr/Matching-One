# A prospective growing-size sign law for original pooled U

## Result

Let the equal-area pair be generated by `5k` and `k(4+3i)`, so

```text
N=25k^2,       L=5k,       Delta=1152/625>0,       m=exp(t).
```

Assume the two-cloud root/sector control stated below and a uniform version
of the already classified first two axis winding shells. Then, as
`k,m -> infinity`,

```text
Ustar/A_N = -(L^2-6L+6)/Delta * m^(-(2L+1)) [1+o(1)]
          = -(25k^2-30k+6)/Delta * m^(-(10k+1)) [1+o(1)],       (1)

A_N=N^(13/8)/2.
```

In particular

```text
sign Ustar = negative,
-log|Ustar|/[k log m] ->10,                                   (2)
```

and the parameter-free normalized transmission prediction is

```text
T_k := -Delta Ustar m^(10k+1)
       /[A_N(25k^2-30k+6)] ->1.                               (3)
```

Equation (3) is deliberately prospective and can fail. It is stronger
than the winding barrier: it includes reciprocal normalization, common
root motion, the next shell and the actual within-geometry denominator.
For `k=1`, its coefficient is `25-30+6=1`, reproducing the exact N25
strong-coupling tail

```text
Ustar/A_25 = -m^-11/Delta+O(m^-13).
```

Thus the growing-k sign is continuous with the completed finite-N25
result rather than a new sign convention.

## 1. The raw barriers do not yet determine U

The exact rank-one minima on the pair are

```text
g_axis=10k-1=2L-1,
g_tilt=14k-1.                                                  (4)
```

At fixed h in a compact positive interval, the restricted rank-one sums
therefore begin as

```text
Z1_axis=m^(-(2L-1))[A_L(h)+m^-2 B_L(h)+...],
Z1_tilt=m^(-(14k-1))[T_k(h)+...],                              (5)

A_L(h)=2L sum_(w=1..L-1) h^(Lw).
```

The axis minimum consists of straight winding stripes. Its reciprocal
identity

```text
A_L(h)=h^N A_L(1/h)                                           (6)
```

makes the h derivative of `A_L/(1+h^N)` vanish at h=1. Hence the raw
barrier `2L-1` is absent from the original E slope. A barrier-only
argument cannot assign either the leading exponent or its sign.

## 2. Common root motion creates the two-power delay

The mesoscopic two-cloud calculation gives

```text
h_root=1+m^-2+O(m^-4)                                         (7)
```

at the precision relevant here. Its sharper `-m^-6` correction affects
the rank-one response only four powers after the raw barrier and therefore
does not alter (1).

Expanding the reciprocal minimum shell from h=1 to (7) produces a factor
`m^-2`. The next rank-one shell in (5), the common one-particle partition
normalization, and this displaced minimum shell all contribute at the
same order `m^(-(2L+1))`. The exact two-shell classification gives their
sum in the axis E derivative:

```text
E_axis,h(h_root)
= -[L^2(L^2-6L+6)/2] m^(-(2L+1))[1+o(1)].                     (8)
```

The three pieces cannot be separated without changing the result: the
minimal-stripe displacement, the `g=2L+1` winding decorations, and the
ordinary `g=2` normalization correction have large cancellations. Their
surviving polynomial is positive for every integer `L>=5`, so (8) is
strictly negative.

This answers the root-motion question precisely:

- root motion changes the visible exponent from `2L-1` to `2L+1` and is
  essential to the coefficient;
- its next mesoscopic correction does not change that leading exponent or
  sign;
- it is not legitimate to evaluate the normalized stripe shell at a
  fixed h and append the root shift afterwards.

## 3. The tilted geometry is exponentially later

The tilted minimum is four k powers beyond the axis minimum. Relative to
the surviving axis response in (8), its raw suppression is

```text
m^[-(14k-1)+(10k+1)] = m^(-(4k-2)).                            (9)
```

Thus it cannot change the leading sign provided its minimizing-state and
decoration amplitudes do not grow by `m^(4k-2)` relative to the axis
polynomial. A sufficient explicit condition is

```text
sup_(h near h_root) |d_h P1_tilt(h)|
=o[L^4 m^(-(2L+1))].                                          (10)
```

This condition is testable from future rank-one shell counts. For any
combinatorial amplitude with `log amplitude=O(k log k)`, it follows for
example when `log m/log k -> infinity`; weaker amplitude bounds permit
weaker growth of m. Merely knowing the tilted barrier does not prove this
uniform statement, so (10) is retained as an assumption rather than
hidden in an O symbol.

## 4. The actual denominator preserves the sign

Let

```text
D_h=(Q_axis,h+Q_tilt,h)/2
```

at the common pooled root. Under sector-odds alignment and vanishing
rank-one mass, each geometry has rank-zero and rank-two weights one half
up to o(1). Their conditional occupation means differ by `N[1+o(1)]`.
Therefore

```text
D_h=N/(2h_root)[1+o(1)]=L^2/2[1+o(1)]>0.                      (11)
```

With `E=1-P1` and the fixed ordered angular convention,

```text
Y_h=(E_axis,h-E_tilt,h)/Delta.
```

Equations (8), (10) and (11) give (1). The denominator cancels the `L^2/2`
factor in (8); it does not change the exponent or sign. Replacing it by a
cross-geometry mixture variance is unnecessary and would not prove (11).

Denominator corrections of relative order `m^-2`, rank-one mass, or sector
odds mismatch enter two or more powers later once (11) is established.
They can only threaten the leading law if the within-geometry denominator
itself becomes anomalously small, which the stated sector assumptions
exclude.

## 5. Exact assumptions and failure modes

The sign/exponent statement uses the following separate inputs.

1. **Common-root sector odds.** At the pooled root,
   `P1_axis+P1_tilt=o(1)` and the restricted cross-ratio
   `Xi=Z2_axis Z0_tilt/(Z0_axis Z2_tilt)` obeys `log Xi=o(1)`. This gives
   (11), not merely equality of total pressures.

2. **Mesoscopic root chart.** The root satisfies (7) uniformly on both
   geometries. A sufficient regime is the previously derived growing-
   systole two-cloud limit; `N/m^4->0` additionally makes the rank-two
   state pure but is not required solely to state (11).

3. **Uniform axis decoration dominance.** Write the exact normalized
   axis h-slope after the first two shells as the term in (8) plus R. The
   required condition is

   ```text
   R=o[L^4 m^(-(2L+1))].                                      (12)
   ```

   Equivalently, if `C_(L,j)` bounds the normalized derivative coefficient
   at shell `g=2L-1+2j`, a directly countable sufficient gate is

   ```text
   sum_(j>=2) C_(L,j) m^(-2(j-1))=o(L^4).                     (13)
   ```

   The fixed-L two-shell theorem alone does not imply this growing-L
   uniformity.

4. **Tilted suppression.** Equation (10) holds. It permits arbitrary
   positive minimizing-state counts below the stated scale; no equality
   of the two geometries' amplitudes is assumed.

Failure of (3) has a sharp interpretation. A sign change at sufficiently
large k,m would require at least one of: nonuniform higher axis decorations,
tilted entropy overcoming `m^(4k-2)`, loss of sector-odds alignment, or a
collapsed within-geometry denominator. It would not by itself refute the
exact rank-one barrier.

## 6. Scale of the unnormalized original U

Since

```text
A_N=(25k^2)^(13/8)/2,
```

equation (1) also predicts

```text
Ustar = -[25^(21/8)/(2 Delta)]
        k^(21/4) m^(-(10k+1))[1+O(k^-1)+o(1)].                 (14)
```

The polynomial `k^(21/4)` cannot compete with the winding exponential
when the amplitude conditions above hold. This is a growing-lattice,
growing-coupling statement, not a fixed-m thermodynamic exponent or a
continuum H4 identification.

## Scientific card

- **Mechanism changed:** the negative tail is an axis rank-one thermal
  slope surviving a two-power reciprocal/root cancellation, not the raw
  minimum winding probability.
- **Prospective prediction:** the normalized quantity (3) tends to one;
  the sign is negative and the logarithmic exponent is 10 per k.
- **Controls retained:** common moving root, separate geometry
  normalization, within-geometry denominator and tilted rank-one sector.
- **Needed new input:** uniform growing-k shell-amplitude bounds (10),
  (12); fixed-k polynomial asymptotics alone are insufficient.
- **Not claimed:** fixed-m behavior, an asymptotic field identity, or a
  sign theorem without the stated sector/decorations gates.
