# P250 adaptive x spatial-spectrum pilot

The 20k N505 common-field pilot completed on HZ in 16.36 seconds. All four
primary/complement typed gates were exact, with zero failures.

The useful result is an amplitude-transfer failure.  The source-frozen
N325/N425 conditional coefficient was `1.78731(1007)`.  N505 gives
`1.28365(4440)` on the plus child and `1.40381(4713)` on the minus child.  In
the stable two-coordinate projection of the frozen profile-zero hypothesis,
the residuals have marginal z scores -10.11 and -7.53 and joint
chi-square=168.66/2 (`p=2.38e-37`).  Thus `Rminus` is not merely the old
medium-geometry amplitude multiplied by the N505 defined-rate profile.

This pilot does **not** resolve a new spatial state.  With only 20 batches the
100-coordinate cone diagnostic has rank 19 and is saturated by its 102
nonnegative weights; its zero distance is uninformative.  A post-reveal,
cluster-preserving randomization of the conditional response over the 101
residues gives `p=0.360`, so no conditional spatial heterogeneity is resolved.
The mechanism increment is therefore scale/geometry dependence of the
adaptive amplitude, not escape from the ordinary positive Fourier cone.

No larger run is licensed by this pilot.  A future spatial-shape experiment
would need many more independently scored batches or a frozen low-dimensional
frequency contrast; increasing replicas inside the same 20 covariance blocks
does not solve the rank gate.
